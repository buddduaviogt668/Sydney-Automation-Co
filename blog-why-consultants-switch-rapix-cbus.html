🏢 Commercial • 📅 May 05, 2026 • ⏱ 5 min read

TL;DR: RAPIX is dominating mid-to-large commercial builds for a reason: lower commissioning time, built-in diagnostics, and seamless C-Bus integration. Here's what Sydney specifiers need to know.

The Problem with Legacy DALI-2 Systems

Traditional DALI requires extensive address mapping, proprietary software, and weeks of on-site programming. RAPIX uses IP-based topology, reducing commissioning time by 60%.

Why Sydney Projects Are Switching

  • Plug-and-Play Topology: No daisy-chain limits, automatic addressing, and real-time fault mapping
  • C-Bus Integration: Existing C-Bus switchboards remain in place; RAPIX handles the lighting network
  • Strata-Ready Logging: Automated compliance reports for NCC 2022 and BCA energy audits

When NOT to Use RAPIX

Small residential retrofits, heritage-listed buildings with strict wiring limits, or sites where the client demands full on-premise control without cloud connectivity.
