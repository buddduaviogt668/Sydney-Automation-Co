Frequently Asked Questions

What is C-Bus programming? +
C-Bus is a professional lighting control system by Clipsal/Schneider Electric. It allows automated control of lights, blinds, and other electrical devices across commercial and residential buildings. We provide C-Bus programming, repairs, and system upgrades across Sydney.
How quickly can you fix a C-Bus system? +
We offer same-day fault finding and emergency repairs across Greater Sydney. Most issues are diagnosed and resolved within 24 hours. Contact us on 0422 469 739 for urgent support.
What areas do you service? +
We service Greater Sydney including Menai, Sutherland Shire, Cronulla, Miranda, Engadine, Kirrawee, North Shore, Eastern Suburbs, and Sydney CBD. We offer mobile callouts and emergency support across all regions.
Are you accredited for DALI-2 and emergency lighting? +
Yes, we are accredited in DALI-2 Compliance, AFSS Emergency Lighting Certification, and Dynalite System Design. We support commercial strata and building compliance across NSW.
