Our Technical Process

The 3-Step Resolution Framework

We don't guess — we diagnose. Our technical approach ensures your lighting system is restored to factory standards, not just "patched up."

1

Deep-Level Diagnosis

We connect our diagnostic laptop running C-Bus Toolkit or Signify Dynalite System Builder. Using a physical Network Analyser, we identify bus voltage drops, clock collisions, and burden errors that general electricians miss.

Site Diagnostic: $155/hr + GST (Min 3 Hrs)
2

Specialist Repair

We replace malfunctioning hardware (relays, dimmers, power supplies) from our stocked service vans. We then fix the underlying code, resolve Pascal Logic errors, and clean up network traffic.

Hardware Swap: Same-Day Available
3

Optimization & Support

We perform a full system health check, update firmware, and backup your network XML file. You receive a digital copy of your programming, ensuring you are never "locked out" of your own system again.

Full Backup: Included in Service

Frequently Asked Questions

What is C-Bus programming? +
C-Bus is a professional lighting control system by Clipsal/Schneider Electric. It allows automated control of lights, blinds, and other electrical devices across commercial and residential buildings. We provide C-Bus programming, repairs, and system upgrades across Sydney.
How quickly can you fix a C-Bus system? +
We offer same-day fault finding and emergency repairs across Greater Sydney. Most issues are diagnosed and resolved within 24 hours. Contact us on 0422 469 739 for urgent support.
What areas do you service? +
We service Greater Sydney including Menai, Sutherland Shire, Cronulla, Miranda, Engadine, Kirrawee, North Shore, Eastern Suburbs, and Sydney CBD. We offer mobile callouts and emergency support across all regions.
Are you accredited for DALI-2 and emergency lighting? +
Yes, we are accredited in DALI-2 Compliance, AFSS Emergency Lighting Certification, and Signify Dynalite System Design. We support commercial strata and building compliance across NSW.
