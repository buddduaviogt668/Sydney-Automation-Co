Why "All-in-One" Smart Homes Fail

Off-the-shelf smart hubs promise simplicity but choke on scale. Commercial-grade lighting control (C-Bus/Dynalite) handles reliability, while HomeKit/Alexa handles convenience. The magic is in the gateway.
