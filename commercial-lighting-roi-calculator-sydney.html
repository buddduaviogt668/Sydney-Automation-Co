Why It Adds Up

Where the Savings Actually Come From

Commercial lighting is typically 25–35% of a building's electricity bill. Most buildings are still wasting 40–70% of that energy.

💡 LED Efficiency

LED fittings use 60–80% less energy than older fluorescent, halogen and HID lamps — with better light quality and dramatically lower maintenance.

⏰ Automation & Scheduling

C-Bus, Dynalite and DALI schedules stop lights running after hours — often worth 15–30% on top of the LED saving alone.

🚶 Occupancy & Daylight

Sensors and daylight harvesting dim or switch off lights in empty or naturally-lit areas — the biggest single control saving in offices and car parks.

FAQs

Commercial Lighting ROI — Common Questions

What does a commercial lighting retrofit actually include? +
A typical scope covers high-efficiency LED fittings (tubes, panels, battens or bay fittings), emergency lighting compliance where required, and lighting control — either a new C-Bus / Dynalite / DALI system or reprogramming and optimisation of an existing one. We produce a written scope and fixed quote before any work begins.
Is a 2-year payback realistic for my building? +
For older fluorescent or halogen buildings running long hours, payback of 2–4 years is common. For buildings with existing LED and controls already in place, savings are smaller and payback will be longer — which is why our calculator factors in your current technology and control level.
Do you install new C-Bus, Dynalite or DALI systems, or just fix them? +
Both. We design and install new systems for new builds and retrofits, and we specialise in recommissioning, upgrading and repairing existing C-Bus, Dynalite and DALI networks across Sydney — including systems other integrators have walked away from.
Can you optimise my existing C-Bus or Dynalite system without replacing it? +
Yes. Many buildings have capable hardware running poorly-configured scenes and schedules. We audit the system, correct the programming and add or repair occupancy and daylight sensors — often unlocking a 10–20% energy saving without new hardware.
Is there any obligation after using this calculator? +
None. The calculator is free, the on-site audit is free, and our written scopes come with a fixed price — you only approve work you want to proceed with.
