Why It Matters

Not Everyone Can
Fix the Network.

Most C-Bus faults are software and network issues — not hardware failures. An electrician without C-Bus Toolkit, Commission Spacelogic and proper accreditation can replace a relay module, but they can't fix an address conflict or restore corrupted programming.

We've inherited dozens of C-Bus systems across Sydney that a well-meaning electrician made worse. The right tool for a C-Bus fault is an Accredited C-Bus Programmer with the right software — not a sparky with a screwdriver.

What we use on every C-Bus job:

  • C-Bus Toolkit (network programming & diagnostics)
  • C-Bus Commission Spacelogic
  • PICED (C-Bus Wiser integration)
  • Schneider Electric authorised software

Our C-Bus background:

Years were spent in national technical support and technical sales roles within Clipsal's C-Bus division before founding Sydney Automation Co. That inside knowledge of how C-Bus is designed, built and fails is what separates us from general automation companies.
