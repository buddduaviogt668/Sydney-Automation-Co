Facility Managers: A Practical Guide to C-Bus, Dynalite and DALI Ma...

Most commercial buildings have lighting control systems that are never properly maintained. A practical framework for Sydney facility managers.

If you manage a commercial building, hospital, educational facility, aged care home, retail centre or government property in Sydney, there is a high probability the building has a Dynalite, C-Bus, RAPIX or DALI lighting control system. There is also a high probability it has not been properly serviced since it was commissioned.

we works with facility managers across Greater Sydney to provide specialist lighting control maintenance, compliance support and fault response for buildings that the original installer may no longer service.

Why Lighting Control Systems Need Specialist Maintenance

Dynalite, C-Bus and RAPIX systems are not like standard electrical infrastructure. They have network topologies, software configurations, and integration points that drift over time — especially in buildings where electrical, HVAC, BMS or AV work is done periodically by third-party contractors. Every time an Automation Specialist does work near a lighting control switchboard without the right knowledge, there is a risk of programming corruption, address conflicts, or integration failures.

The Four Things Facility Managers Should Know

First, know what system you have. Is it Dynalite, C-Bus, RAPIX, or a combination? Each platform requires different tools and accreditation to service properly. Second, know whether a current programming backup exists. A single hardware failure or power event without a backup could wipe all programmed scenes. Third, understand your emergency lighting compliance obligations under NCC 2022 DALI-2 requirements. Fourth, have a specialist on call before a fault occurs — not after.
