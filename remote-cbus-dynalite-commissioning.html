How It Works

Remote Commissioning Process

01

Tell Us What You Need

Call or email with the scope: system type (C-Bus/Dynalite), what needs programming, and when you need it done. We confirm if remote is suitable (it almost always is for programming work).

02

Prepare the Connection

You need: a laptop with C-Bus Toolkit or Dynalite software installed, connected to the C-Bus network via USB or ethernet interface, and an internet connection. We use TeamViewer or AnyDesk — whichever you prefer.

03

We Remote In & Commission

We connect, take control of the commissioning software, and perform all programming. You can watch the entire session. We explain each step as we go. Typical programming sessions take 30–90 minutes.

04

Verify & Sign Off

We test all functions, confirm the network is stable, and provide documentation. You test on-site that everything works as expected. We're available for follow-up if needed.
