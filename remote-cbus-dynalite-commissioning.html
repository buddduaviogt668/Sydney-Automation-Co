How It Works

Remote Commissioning Process

01

Tell Us What You Need

Call or email with the platform, project location, required change and the person who can access the system on-site. We confirm whether remote delivery is suitable before scheduling.

02

Prepare the Connection

You need: a laptop with C-Bus Toolkit or Dynalite software installed, connected to the C-Bus network via USB or ethernet interface, and an internet connection. We agree the remote-access method and connection responsibilities before the session.

03

We Remote In & Commission

We connect, take control of the commissioning software, and perform all programming. You can watch the entire session. We explain each step as we go. The session length depends on the scope, platform and condition of the existing project file.

04

Verify & Sign Off

We test all functions, confirm the network is stable, and provide documentation. You test on-site that everything works as expected. We're available for follow-up if needed.
