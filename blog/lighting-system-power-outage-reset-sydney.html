Lighting System Crashed After Power Outage in Sydney? Your Reset Guide

Published: 2026-06-12 | Updated: 2026-07-14 | Author: George Skarmoutsos, Accredited C-Bus Programmer & Dynalite System Designer

After a power outage, C-Bus systems require a 30-second power cycle of the 5500PS power supply followed by a 2-minute network re-initialisation. Dynalite DDBC1200 processors need the same 30-second power disconnect. DALI devices typically auto-recover. If any system has lost programming due to a power surge, restore from your database backup — which Sydney Automation Co. provides on every service visit.

What Happens to Your Lighting Control During a Power Outage

When mains power drops, your lighting control system loses its active communication state. While both C-Bus and Dynalite processors store their programming in non-volatile memory (meaning your scenes, schedules, and configurations should survive a normal outage), the communication network between devices needs to re-establish itself when power returns.

The critical distinction is between a clean power outage (mains simply drops and returns) and a power surge during restoration (voltage spike that can corrupt processor memory or damage modules). Most post-outage failures fall into three categories: communication not re-initialised, processor locked up, or memory corruption from a surge.

C-Bus System Reset After Power Outage

  1. Wait for stable mains power — Do not touch any C-Bus equipment while power is fluctuating. Wait until mains voltage is stable for at least 2 minutes.
  2. Power cycle the 5500PS power supply — Turn off the circuit breaker feeding the C-Bus power supply. Wait 30 seconds. Turn it back on.
  3. Wait for network re-initialisation — The 5500PC network interface takes approximately 2 minutes to re-establish C-Bus network communication. Watch for the status LEDs to return to normal (steady operation, not rapid flashing).
  4. Test keypads and scenes — Press each keypad button to verify channel response. Test all programmed scenes to confirm logic is intact.
  5. If a module is unresponsive — A specific C-Bus module (dimmer or relay) that fails to rejoin the network may need its own power cycle at the circuit breaker.

Dynalite System Reset After Power Outage

  1. Check processor status — Look at the LED indicators on the DDBC1200 processor. A normal boot sequence shows specific LED patterns. Error LEDs indicate a fault.
  2. Power cycle the processor — Disconnect power for 30 seconds, then reconnect. The DyNet bus re-initialisation takes approximately 2 minutes.
  3. Verify DyNet bus voltage — Measure between DyNet+ and DyNet- at any keypad. Should read up to 16V DC (data lines 0–3V). Low voltage indicates the power supply may have been damaged by the surge.
  4. Test all keypads — Walk through each room and test every button. If a keypad is unresponsive, see our Dynalite keypad troubleshooting guide.
  5. Check schedules and timeclocks — Dynalite timeclocks may drift after a prolonged power outage. Verify all time-based schedules are correct in System Builder.

DALI System Recovery After Power Outage

  1. DALI devices auto-recover — Unlike C-Bus and Dynalite, DALI bus devices are designed to automatically rejoin the bus after power restoration.
  2. Check DALI bus power supplies — Verify all DALI bus power supplies are active and providing correct voltage.
  3. Test emergency lighting — Power outages can trigger DALI emergency lighting test cycles. Check that all emergency luminaires report correct status and that no fault flags have been raised.
  4. Verify DALI-2 integration — If your DALI system integrates with a BMS, verify the BMS communication link has re-established.
