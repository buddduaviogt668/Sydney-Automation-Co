Frequently Asked Questions

What types of backlighting do you install? +
We install LED strip lighting, panel lighting, and custom LED solutions for both residential and commercial applications. All systems can be controlled via C-Bus for precise dimming and scene management.
Can backlighting be controlled by C-Bus? +
Yes. We integrate backlighting with C-Bus dimming modules for precise brightness control. You can create scenes that combine backlighting with your main lighting for perfect ambiance.
Do you design custom backlighting solutions? +
Yes. We work with architects, interior designers, and business owners to create bespoke backlighting designs. From initial concept through to installation and programming.
