Why Sydney Automation Co. for Northern Beaches?

The Northern Beaches has a strong concentration of prestige residential C-Bus installs — particularly across Manly, Newport and Palm Beach. Coastal salt air and proximity to water accelerates corrosion on C-Bus terminations and relay module contacts. We also service a number of apartment buildings across the Beaches where Dynalite systems manage common area and lobby lighting. If your system has been impacted by humidity, a power event, or electrical works — we'll diagnose and fix it.
