Why Sydney Automation Co. for Sydney CBD?

The Sydney CBD has the highest concentration of Dynalite-controlled buildings in Australia. Commercial office buildings, five-star hotels, and mixed-use towers depend on Dynalite for lobby, atrium, conference and tenant lighting. C-Bus is also prevalent in smaller CBD fitouts, boutique office buildings, and residential towers. After-hours callouts, building manager support, and strata management relationships are a normal part of our CBD work.
