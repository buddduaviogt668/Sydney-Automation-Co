What We Fix

C-Bus Faults We
Repair Every Day

Most C-Bus faults are caused by a small set of recurring failures. As accredited specialists, we diagnose and repair all of them — usually on the first visit.

  • Dimmer module failures — lights not responding or flickering
  • C-Bus network communication errors (no response on network)
  • Keypad and input unit failures — buttons not working
  • Relay and output unit faults — circuits stuck on or off
  • Programming corruption — scenes and schedules not triggering
  • Power supply unit failures
  • C-Bus PC Interface (PCIA) and CNI faults
  • Logic gate and trigger programming errors
  • C-Bus touchscreen (C-Touch) faults
  • Post-renovation system damage and rewiring faults

FAQs

C-Bus Repair Questions Answered

How much does C-Bus repair cost in Sydney? +
C-Bus repair costs vary by fault type and time on-site. Most diagnostic visits are 1-2 hours. Simple faults like keypad replacements or power supply issues are typically resolved on the first visit. Network-wide and programming faults may require additional time. We provide a clear scope and quote before any work proceeds — no surprises.
Can you repair C-Bus systems installed by someone else? +
Yes — the majority of our C-Bus repair work is on systems installed by other electricians. We are Clipsal C-Bus accredited and can access, diagnose and repair any C-Bus installation regardless of who originally installed or programmed it.
How quickly can you respond to a C-Bus fault in Sydney? +
We offer same-day response for urgent C-Bus faults across Greater Sydney. Call 0422 469 739 to check current availability. Priority bookings are available for commercial buildings and strata complexes.
What are the most common C-Bus faults you repair? +
The most common C-Bus faults we see in Sydney: dimmer module failures, C-Bus network communication errors, keypad and input unit failures, relay and output unit faults, programming corruption, and power supply failures. Most are diagnosed and repaired on the first visit.
Do you carry C-Bus replacement parts? +
We carry the most common C-Bus replacement modules and components. For less common or discontinued units, we source parts quickly through our Clipsal supply chain. We'll advise on part availability before you commit to a repair.
Can you upgrade my C-Bus system while repairing it? +
Yes. Many clients take the opportunity to upgrade scenes, schedules, dimmer curves or add automation features while we're on-site for a repair. This is cost-effective since the system is already open and diagnosed. Ask us what's possible for your install.
