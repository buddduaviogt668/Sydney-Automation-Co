How It Works

Your Lighting, On Any Screen.
Powered by 5500AC2.

The Clipsal 5500AC2 Wiser for C-Bus Application Controller is a DIN-rail mounted device that plugs into your existing C-Bus switchboard. It hosts a fully custom web-based lighting visualisation interface — floor plans, room controls, dimmer sliders, scenes, and schedules — served directly to any device on your network.

You then wall-mount an iPad or Samsung Galaxy Tab using the existing cable run from your old 5000CT panel. The existing wall power is reused to charge the tablet. The result is a premium, modern, fully customised touch panel at a fraction of the cost of proprietary hardware.

The Hardware

What Is the 5500AC2 Wiser Application Controller?

The professional-grade C-Bus controller that makes iPad control possible.

🌐

Web-Based Interface

Hosts a fully custom web visualisation directly — floor plans, room grids, scene buttons, dimmer sliders. Accessible in any browser on iPad, Samsung Galaxy Tab, iPhone, or laptop. No proprietary apps required.

🔗

Talks to Your Existing C-Bus

Connects directly to your existing C-Bus network via the onboard CNI (Ethernet gateway). Your existing relays, dimmers, keypads, and sensors all continue to work exactly as before. Zero rewiring.

🏠

Apple HomeKit, Google & Alexa

Native support for Apple HomeKit, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa. Control lighting with Siri, set location-based automations, and run scenes from any Apple device — in addition to the tablet wall panel.

🔒

Remote Access — Anywhere

Access your full lighting interface from anywhere in the world — holiday, office, or airport. No VPN required. Secure cloud connectivity via the C-Bus Manager app (firmware V2.1.0+).

⚙️

Advanced Logic & Scheduling

Powered by a Lua-based logic engine. Complex automations, time-based schedules, sunrise/sunset triggers, and conditional logic — all programmed without proprietary hardware panels.

📈

Up to 4,000 Objects

Supports up to 4,000 network objects — ample for large residential properties and commercial suites. Modbus (10 devices) and BACnet IP (50 point server) for broader building integration.

Our Process

How We Replace Your Touch Screen — Step by Step

1

Site Assessment

We inspect your existing C-Bus network, identify your current hardware (5000CT, 5080CTC, SC5000CTL2, or C-5000CTDL2), assess the switchboard, and confirm the cable runs to your existing wall panels. We provide a fixed-price quote on the spot.

2

5500AC2 Installation & Network Configuration

We install the 5500AC2 Wiser Application Controller on your DIN rail. We connect it to your C-Bus network and configure it with your existing group addresses, scenes, and schedules. All current programming is preserved.

3

Custom Interface Design

We design your custom web visualisation — your floor plan, labelled room controls, dimmer sliders, scene buttons (Evening, Movie, Away, Morning), and schedule views. The interface is designed to match your home layout and how you actually use your lighting.

4

Tablet Wall Mount & Cabling

We remove your old 5000CT panel and repurpose the existing wall power to a modern flush-mount tablet cradle (iPad or Samsung Galaxy Tab). The result is a clean, professional wall panel — often in exactly the same wall location as your old screen.

5

HomeKit, Google & Remote Access Setup

We configure Apple HomeKit integration, Google Home, and remote cloud access so you can control your lighting from anywhere. We also set up the C-Bus Manager app on your phone for off-site control.

6

Handover & Training

We walk you through every feature, hand over all credentials, and provide 12-month programming warranty on all our work. If you ever need a scene added, a schedule changed, or a new room added — a single phone call sorts it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I replace just the touch screen without changing anything else? +
Yes. The 5500AC2 connects to your existing C-Bus network. Your existing relays, dimmers, switches, and sensors all remain in place and continue to work normally. We only add the 5500AC2 controller to your switchboard and replace the physical wall panel.
Will my existing C-Bus scenes and schedules still work? +
Yes. During installation we export your existing C-Bus configuration and migrate all group addresses, scene settings, and schedules into the 5500AC2. You won't lose any of your current programming.
What happens if my iPad breaks or becomes outdated? +
You simply replace the tablet. The 5500AC2 controller stays in the switchboard and your interface design is saved. A new iPad or Samsung tablet connects immediately — no reprogramming required. This is the core advantage over proprietary touch panels like the old 5000CT.
How long does the upgrade take? +
Most residential upgrades (1 to 3 old 5000CT screens) are completed in a single day. Larger properties with more complex programming may require 2 days. We'll give you a precise timeframe during the site assessment.
Can I control lighting from my phone when I'm not at home? +
Yes. The 5500AC2 supports secure remote access via the Clipsal C-Bus Manager app (iOS and Android). You can control all scenes and lighting groups from anywhere. If you also set up Apple HomeKit, your iPhone's Home app works for remote control too.
Do you service properties that had their 5000CT installed by another company? +
Absolutely. We service all C-Bus systems regardless of who originally installed them. We work with SC5000CTL2 and C-5000CTDL2 systems across Sydney every week. Bring us the problem — we solve it.
