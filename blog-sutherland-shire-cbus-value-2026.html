TL;DR: Not all smart home upgrades move the needle on property value. In the Shire, C-Bus reliability, energy zoning, and compliance-ready lighting control consistently appraise higher than gimmicky app-based hubs.

What Buyers in Menai & Sutherland Actually Care About

Post-2023 buyers prioritize energy efficiency, low maintenance, and systems that don't require a subscription. C-Bus delivers all three — if programmed correctly.

Upgrades That Pay Off

  • Zoned DALI/C-Bus Dimming: 15-20% energy savings, instantly noticeable in strata reports
  • Scene-Based Automation: "Entertaining", "Security", "Morning" scenes that work without phones
  • Future-Proof Rough-In: Cat6 to switchboards, spare DIN space, and labelled DALI circuits

What to Avoid

Wi-Fi-only dimmers, cloud-dependent hubs, or "smart" switches that require firmware updates to stay secure. Reliability beats novelty in the Shire market.
