Our Work

Projects —
Commercial C-Bus & Dynalite Across Sydney.

A selection of commercial projects completed by Sydney Automation Co. — C-Bus upgrades, commissioning and automation across Greater Sydney.

The Rocks
NSW Government Placemaking Offices

C-Bus & DALI Lighting Recommissioning

Our recent commissioning upgrade project at the NSW Government Placemaking Offices in The Rocks involved the recommissioning and reprogramming of new DALI lighting fittings integrated with the Clipsal C-Bus system. The upgraded lighting control system was configured through Clipsal C-Bus EDLTs across office boardrooms, kitchen areas, quiet rooms, and reception spaces to ensure seamless operation and improved functionality throughout the workspace.

Ingleburn
Kebia Importex

C-Bus SpaceLogic Relay Upgrade & Modernization

A faulty 12-channel C-Bus relay was causing lights to flicker and behave erratically across warehouse areas and cool rooms. Diagnostics confirmed an internal power supply failure. We replaced the legacy unit with a brand-new 16-channel SpaceLogic relay, providing the client with updated technology and the flexibility to add more circuits in the future.

North Sydney
Winten Property Group

C-Bus SpaceLogic Relay Upgrade

Existing C-Bus relay modules across the office were malfunctioning, causing lights to randomly switch on and off throughout all office areas. All relay units were replaced with new Schneider Electric C-Bus SpaceLogic 16-channel relay modules, resolving the fault completely.

Parramatta
WSU — 1 Parramatta Square

Automated Blind Control Programming & Commissioning

Engaged by the blinds contractor to program and commission new automated sheer and blockout blind control across 3 floors in the building at WSU's 1 Parramatta Square campus. Scope covered EDLT configuration and blind controller commissioning — programming automated scenes and schedules for the new blind system.

Parramatta South
WSU — EB Building, School of Nursing & Midwifery

Dynalite System Minor Works & Reprogramming

Engaged by the site electricians to carry out minor service and reprogramming works on the Dynalite lighting control system at WSU's Parramatta South Campus — EB Building, School of Nursing & Midwifery. Works included Dynalite switch replacement, system reprogramming and recommissioning across multiple rooms on campus.

Yulara, NT
Uluru Meeting Place — Ayers Rock Resort

DALI Lighting Upgrade — Remote Commissioning at Ayers Rock Resort

Sydney Automation Co. was engaged by Dream Tech AV to program and commission a new DALI lighting installation in the Ballrooms at the iconic Uluru Meeting Place, Ayers Rock Resort. The project involved replacing older DSI fittings with modern DALI LED drivers, creating new lighting scenes across the ballroom and foyer areas with full room join functionality — all delivered completely remotely from Sydney to Uluru. Remote commissioning at one of Australia's most remote and culturally significant landmarks presented unique challenges, from coordinating with the onsite electrician and AV team across time zones to troubleshooting network connectivity over distance. This landmark project demonstrates that Sydney Automation Co. can deliver complex lighting control outcomes anywhere in Australia, no matter how remote.

Sydney CBD
Tank Stream Labs — 333 Kent St, Merchant House

Dynalite Lighting Control — Reprogramming at 333 Kent St, Sydney CBD

Sydney Automation Co. was engaged by the onsite electrical contractor to deliver a lighting control project at Tank Stream Labs — the co-working and innovation hub at 333 Kent Street, in the heart of the Sydney CBD. With practical completion and client handover scheduled for the following day, the project required a fast, reliable solution under tight time constraints. Our team responded quickly, reprogramming the Signify Dynalite lighting control system and implementing the required changes to ensure everything was fully operational and ready for handover. Working in an occupied CBD commercial building brought its own coordination challenges — but the system was left fully tested and commissioned on time. Another successful project delivered, demonstrating our commitment to efficient, dependable lighting control for commercial clients across Sydney.
