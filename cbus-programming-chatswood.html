What does C-Bus programming involve in a Chatswood home? +
C-Bus programming in a typical Chatswood home involves setting up lighting scenes (Morning, Away, Entertain), configuring time-based schedules, programming keypad button functions, setting dimmer curves, and integrating with Apple HomeKit or other smart home platforms. We provide full project documentation on completion.
My C-Bus was installed but never properly programmed in Chatswood — can you help? +
Absolutely — this is very common. Many C-Bus systems are installed by electricians who don't specialise in programming, leaving the system in a basic or default state. We access your existing installation, back up the current project, and reprogram to match how you actually use your home.
How long does C-Bus reprogramming take for a Chatswood home? +
Most residential C-Bus reprogramming jobs in Chatswood take 3–6 hours depending on the number of zones, scenes required and integration complexity. We quote a fixed scope before starting. Commercial jobs are assessed on-site.
Do you send your own technicians to Chatswood, or subcontractors? +
We send our own accredited technicians to all Chatswood jobs — no subcontractors. The person who answers the phone is the same business that turns up at your door. All our technicians hold current Clipsal C-Bus accreditation.
Do you provide documentation after a Chatswood job? +
Yes. Every job includes a written record of work carried out, faults identified and any recommendations. For strata and commercial clients we provide documentation suitable for body corporate and facilities management records.
