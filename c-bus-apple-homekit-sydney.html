C-Bus HomeKit FAQs

Do I need to replace my C-Bus hardware to use Apple HomeKit? +
No. The integration is done via a bridge or gateway device that connects your existing C-Bus network to HomeKit. Your C-Bus switches, relay modules and dimmer units all stay in place.
Will the C-Bus switches still work normally after HomeKit integration? +
Yes â€” HomeKit integration is additive. Your existing C-Bus wall switches and keypads continue to work exactly as before. HomeKit simply adds another way to control the same lighting groups.
Can I control C-Bus from outside my home with HomeKit? +
Yes, with an Apple TV (4th gen or later), HomePod or iPad set up as a home hub in your home. This acts as a relay so you can control your C-Bus lighting from anywhere via the Apple Home app.
What C-Bus hardware do I need for HomeKit integration? +
You'll need either a C-Bus network interface (CNI) connected to your local network, or a Wiser for C-Bus unit already installed. Most C-Bus systems installed in recent years have one of these. We'll confirm during the assessment.
Can you integrate C-Bus with Google Home or Amazon Alexa as well? +
Yes â€” if you'd like Google Home or Alexa control in addition to or instead of HomeKit, we can set that up as well. Ask us about multi-platform integration when you get in touch.
