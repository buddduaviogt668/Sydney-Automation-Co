Sutherland Shire FAQ

How quickly can you get to the Sutherland Shire?
Because we're based in Menai, same-day callouts across the Shire are standard — usually 1–2 hours from your call. No waiting days for a technician to drive from the CBD.
Do you cover all of the Sutherland Shire?
Yes — from Menai and Sutherland in the north to Cronulla and Kurnell in the east, Engadine in the west, and everything in between. We know the Shire intimately.
What makes Shire C-Bus faults different?
The coastal humidity in suburbs like Cronulla and Caringbah accelerates corrosion on C-Bus terminations. Many Shire homes have 15–20 year old C-Bus installations that need hardware updates. We understand both of these local factors.
Can you work with my electrician?
Absolutely. Sydney Automation Co. is not an electrical contractor — there's zero competitive overlap. We work alongside electrical contractors on new builds, renovations and commercial projects across the Shire all the time.
