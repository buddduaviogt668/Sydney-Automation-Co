My C-Bus lights randomly drop out in my Killara home — what could cause this?

Intermittent C-Bus dropouts in large Upper North Shore homes are most commonly caused by failing power supply units, overloaded C-Bus segments, or loose terminals in older installations. George has specialist diagnostic tools to isolate the cause — often in a single visit after other electricians have failed.

Can George set up Apple HomeKit control in my Killara home via C-Bus?

What is the difference between C-Bus and regular smart home automation?

C-Bus is a professional-grade, hardwired lighting control system installed at time of build — it is significantly more reliable and capable than consumer smart home products like Philips Hue or LIFX. However, it requires specialist programming to reconfigure. George is one of Sydney's few accredited independent C-Bus programmers.
