Signs of Failure

Is Your C-Bus Relay or Power Supply Failing?

🔊

Buzzing or Humming Relay

A loud buzz or chatter from the relay enclosure indicates the contactor is vibrating rather than latching. This is a fire risk — the relay can fuse closed. Needs immediate replacement.

💡

Lights Stuck On or Off

A zone of lights that won't turn off (or on) is the classic symptom of a failed relay output. The internal mechanism has welded or broken. Common in L5504RSF and 5104D20RS units.

⚡

Flickering or Dimming

Unstable power delivery from a failing power supply unit can cause intermittent flickering, dimming, or erratic behaviour across multiple C-Bus zones.

🚫

Entire Zone Dead

A completely unresponsive lighting zone — no power, no response from C-Bus Toolkit — points to a failed relay or its power supply. The unit needs replacement, not reprogramming.

How It Works

Our Replacement Process

01

Diagnose the Fault

We use C-Bus Toolkit to identify the failed unit. Relay stuck closed? Power supply voltage drop? Network communication loss? We pinpoint the exact cause.

02

Source the Correct Replacement

We carry common units in stock (L5504RSF, L5504PSF, 5104D20RS). For older or discontinued models, we source compatible replacements including NOS (new old stock).

03

Replace & Reprogram

We install the replacement, reconfigure group addresses and scenes in C-Bus Toolkit, and verify all loads operate correctly. No licensed electrical work needed — a qualified electrician handles the wiring; we handle the automation.

04

Full System Test

We test all affected zones, confirm the replacement is communicating on the C-Bus network, and verify programming. We provide documentation for strata records or building management.
