Important pricing clarity

The $10k starting point is not a blank cheque.

It is a transparent entry point for a defined package. A home that needs new cabling, substantial electrical work or a large amount of hardware will require a larger budget. That is why we assess the home before recommending a final scope.

Usually additional: major rewiring, new switchboards, builder’s work, extensive hardware quantities, motorised blinds, HVAC, security, distributed audio and complex third-party integrations.

Questions before we talk?

Smart-home planning, without the sales fog.

What does the $10,000 smart-home package include? ＋
The starting package is designed for a three-bedroom home and can include system design, C-Bus or Dynalite programming, practical scenes and schedules, commissioning and handover. Hardware quantities, electrical work and third-party integrations are confirmed after a site assessment.
Does the $10,000 price include rewiring? ＋
No. The starting figure is an indicative package point for design, programming, commissioning and a defined system scope. New cabling, major rewiring, switchboard work, building works and extensive hardware are priced separately when required.
How much does a more advanced smart home cost? ＋
A more complete three-bedroom installation commonly moves into the $18,000 to $25,000 range or higher when it includes more hardware, extensive scenes, multiple systems, motorised blinds, audio-visual integration, voice control or substantial commissioning time.
Can you work with C-Bus or Dynalite? ＋
Yes. Sydney Automation Co. designs and programs residential systems using Clipsal C-Bus and Philips Signify Dynalite, and can assess existing systems before recommending an upgrade, integration or new installation path.
