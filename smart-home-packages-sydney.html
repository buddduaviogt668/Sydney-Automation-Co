Important pricing clarity

The $10k starting point is not a blank cheque.

It is a transparent entry point for a defined package. A home that needs new cabling, substantial electrical work or a large amount of hardware will require a larger budget. That is why we assess the home before recommending a final scope.

Usually additional: major rewiring, new switchboards, builder’s work, extensive hardware quantities, motorised blinds, HVAC, security, distributed audio and complex third-party integrations.

Questions before we talk?

Smart-home planning, without the sales fog.

Can you work with an existing C-Bus or Dynalite system?

Yes. We can assess the existing system, determine what can be retained, identify faults or limitations, and recommend a staged upgrade or integration path where appropriate.

Will you install the electrical wiring as well?

We can identify the wiring and electrical requirements and coordinate the specialist programming and commissioning scope. New cabling, electrical installation and builder’s work are assessed separately and may involve your electrician or project team.

Can I add Alexa, Siri or Google Home?

Voice control can be considered as part of a broader integration plan. The suitability, reliability and cost depend on the selected platform, network, existing controllers and the level of control you want.

What happens after I submit an enquiry?

We will discuss the home, the project stage, the systems you are considering and your priorities. If the project is a fit, we can arrange the next step for a site assessment or structured scope discussion.
