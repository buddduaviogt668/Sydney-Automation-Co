What is Signify Dynalite lighting control? +
Signify Dynalite is a professional lighting control system by Philips/Signify used in commercial, hospitality, and high-end residential buildings across Sydney and Australia.

Can you fix a Signify Dynalite system that has stopped working? +
Yes, we specialise in Signify Dynalite fault finding and repairs across Sydney. We offer same-day callouts for urgent faults.
What is the difference between Signify Dynalite and C-Bus? +
C-Bus is made by Clipsal/Schneider Electric and is common in residential and commercial buildings. Signify Dynalite is made by Philips/Signify and is more common in hospitality and large commercial projects. Both are professional lighting control systems.
