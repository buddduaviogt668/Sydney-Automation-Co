FAQs

Automation Questions
Answered by Our Specialists

What home automation solutions do you install in Sydney? +
We design and install lighting and building automation on Clipsal C-Bus, Philips Dynalite and DALI-2 platforms. This includes smart home voice integration with Apple HomeKit, Google Home and Amazon Alexa, scene and schedule control, sensor-based automation, and whole-of-home dimming and mood lighting.
Can you automate my existing C-Bus or Dynalite lighting without rewiring? +
Yes. In most cases we can modernise an existing C-Bus or Dynalite installation by reprogramming the network, upgrading obsolete relays and power supplies, and adding smart connectivity — without pulling new wiring. We also provide network upgrades and remote commissioning.
Do you provide automation solutions for strata buildings and commercial offices? +
Yes. We automate and maintain common-area lighting, corridors, car parks and facades in strata blocks, plus boardrooms, open-plan offices and tenancy fit-outs in commercial buildings. Our work supports AFSS and emergency egress compliance, and we hold service contracts with strata managers and facility managers across Sydney.
How does voice control integrate with a lighting automation system? +
We integrate C-Bus and Dynalite networks with Apple HomeKit, Google Home and Amazon Alexa using manufacturer-approved gateways and bridges. This lets you control scenes, dimming and schedules by voice or phone app, while the core lighting network keeps operating on its own.
What is involved in upgrading an older C-Bus or Dynalite system? +
We assess the existing bus network, replace obsolete relays and power supplies, update firmware, and reprogram scenes, schedules and controls to current requirements. Upgrades are quoted after a full on-site diagnostic, and no work proceeds without your approval.
