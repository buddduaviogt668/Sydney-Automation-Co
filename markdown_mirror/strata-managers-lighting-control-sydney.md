Strata Managers: Who to Call When Your Building’s Lighting Control ...
======================================================================

The lobby lights are stuck on full and the Automation Specialist says it’s a “computer thing”. Here’s who to call and what to expect.

You have a lighting control fault in a managed building. The after-hours Automation Specialist has been on site, cannot fix it, and the residents are complaining. This is one of the most common scenarios George encounters across Sydney strata buildings — and it’s almost always avoidable with the right specialist on speed dial.

**Why a General Automation Specialist Cannot Fix a [Dynalite](https://www.signify.com/en-au/brands/dynalite) or [C-Bus](https://www.se.com/au/en/work/products/product-launch/cbus/) Fault**

Lighting control systems like Dynalite and C-Bus are specialist platforms. They have their own proprietary software, network addressing protocols, and commissioning tools. A general Automation Specialist — even a good one — cannot diagnose a Dynalite RS485 network dropout or recover lost C-Bus scene programming without the right software and accreditation. It is not a reflection on their skills; it is simply outside their scope.

George is an Accredited C-Bus Programmer and Accredited Dynalite System Designer. He carries the official Clipsal C-Bus Toolkit, C-Bus Commission Spacelogic, and Dynalite commissioning software on every site visit. These are the only tools that can properly diagnose and repair faults on these systems.

**The Most Common Strata Lighting Control Faults**

Across Sydney strata buildings — from Eastern Suburbs apartments to North Shore residential towers and CBD mixed-use developments — the faults George sees most often are: controller dropouts on Dynalite RS485 networks, usually triggered by power events; C-Bus address conflicts introduced by Automation Specialists doing switchboard or tenancy work; scene programming loss after power failures; and emergency lighting non-compliance with [NCC 2022 DALI-2](https://ncc.abcb.gov.au/) requirements.

**What to Expect When You Call Us**

George provides a same-day response for urgent building faults across Greater Sydney. On arrival, he assesses the system, identifies the root cause, and provides a written quote before any repair work begins. All completed work is documented with a service report suitable for your records or owners corporation minutes.
