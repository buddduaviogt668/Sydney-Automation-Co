Common Problems We Solve — What Goes Wrong in Managed Buildings
---------------------------------------------------------------

These are the lighting control problems strata and facility managers contact us about most.

### 🔦 Lighting Faults in Common Areas

Lobby, car park, corridors, stairwells, plant rooms — when the lighting control system fails, it becomes your problem. We diagnose and fix C-Bus and Dynalite faults in common areas fast.

### 🚨 Emergency Lighting Compliance

NCC 2022 DALI-2 compliance requirements for emergency lighting are catching out strata buildings and commercial properties across Sydney. We handle audit, rectification and documentation.

### ⚡ System Failures After Electrical Work

Automation Specialists doing switchboard or tenancy work can inadvertently corrupt C-Bus or Dynalite programming. We're regularly called in to recover systems after electrical work.

### 🏚️ Ageing Systems with No Support

Many Sydney strata buildings have Dynalite or C-Bus systems commissioned during construction and never serviced. If the original integrator is gone, we step in.

### 🏢 Multi-Tenancy Lighting Conflicts

In mixed-use and commercial buildings, tenant fit-out works frequently cause conflicts on shared lighting networks. We untangle address conflicts and restore zoning.

### 🔌 BMS Integration Issues

Dynalite and C-Bus systems integrated with building management platforms can lose their gateway configuration after updates or hardware changes. We restore and re-commission.
