DALI Faults We Fix

DALI Faults in Commercial Buildings
-----------------------------------

DALI is the standard for addressable commercial lighting control in Sydney. Bus faults, driver failures and compliance issues are the most common problems we're called to fix.

* DALI bus short circuit — single driver taking down the whole bus
* DALI address conflicts from incorrect or partial commissioning
* Failed DALI LED drivers and ballasts
* DALI controller and gateway failures
* Emergency lighting test failures under AS/NZS 62386
* NCC 2022 DALI-2 compliance for new commercial builds
* DALI zone expansion and recommissioning after renovation
* Integration faults between DALI and C-Bus or Dynalite

NCC 2022 Compliance

DALI-2 Compliance for NSW Buildings
-----------------------------------

The NCC 2022 introduced stricter DALI-2 requirements for emergency lighting in commercial buildings in NSW. Non-compliant systems can fail building certification and create significant liability.

### What NCC 2022 Requires

* DALI-2 compliant emergency lighting in new Class 5-9 buildings
* Addressable emergency luminaires with test & monitoring capability
* Proper commissioning documentation
* Annual compliance testing records

### How We Help

We assess, commission, test and document your DALI-2 emergency lighting system to ensure it meets NCC 2022 requirements. We also repair failing systems ahead of certification or annual testing.

FAQs

DALI Repair Questions Answered
------------------------------

What is DALI-2 and why does it matter for NSW buildings? +

DALI-2 (Digital Addressable Lighting Interface, version 2) is the international standard for addressable lighting control in commercial buildings. Under NCC 2022, new commercial buildings in NSW must use DALI-2 compliant emergency lighting systems. Non-compliant systems can fail building certification.

What are the most common DALI faults in commercial buildings? +

Common DALI faults: bus short circuits caused by faulty drivers, address conflicts from incorrect commissioning, failed DALI LED drivers, DALI controller failures, and emergency lighting compliance failures during annual testing. Bus shorts are the most disruptive — a single faulty driver can take down an entire zone.

Can you recommission DALI systems after renovation work? +

Yes. Adding new luminaires, replacing drivers, or extending a DALI zone all require recommissioning to address the new devices correctly. We carry out DALI commissioning and recommissioning for commercial projects across Sydney.

We failed our annual emergency lighting test — can you help? +

Yes. Emergency lighting test failures on DALI systems are usually caused by failed drivers, addressing errors, or missing test configuration. We diagnose the cause, repair the fault and provide the documentation needed to demonstrate compliance.
