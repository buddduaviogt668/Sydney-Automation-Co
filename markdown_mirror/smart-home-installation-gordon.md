FAQs

Smart Home Installation Gordon — Your Questions Answered
--------------------------------------------------------

What smart home system should I install in my Gordon home? +

For Gordon residential properties, Clipsal C-Bus remains the gold standard for whole-home lighting control — especially if you're doing a full renovation. For smaller apartments or single-floor upgrades, a DALI or DALI-2 system may offer better value. We provide a free site assessment and system recommendation before any work is scoped.

Do you handle C-Bus and Dynalite for Gordon apartment buildings? +

Yes — we work with strata managers, body corporates and developers on C-Bus and Dynalite installations in Gordon's apartment buildings. This includes common area lighting control, carpark automation, lobby lighting systems and individual apartment fit-outs.

Can you upgrade an older C-Bus system in my Gordon home? +

Yes. Many Gordon homes have C-Bus systems installed during renovations between 2000 and 2015 that can be significantly upgraded in capability without replacing the underlying hardware. We assess your current installation and advise on the most cost-effective upgrade path.

Do you send your own technicians to Gordon, or subcontractors? +

We send our own accredited technicians to all Gordon jobs — no subcontractors. The person who answers the phone is the same business that turns up at your door. All our technicians hold current Clipsal C-Bus accreditation.

Do you provide documentation after a Gordon job? +

Yes. Every job includes a written record of work carried out, faults identified and any recommendations. For strata and commercial clients we provide documentation suitable for body corporate and facilities management records.
