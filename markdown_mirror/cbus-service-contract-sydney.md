C-Bus Service Contract — What's Included
----------------------------------------

A C-Bus service contract replaces unpredictable reactive repair costs with a fixed annual investment in system health. It's more cost-effective, gives you priority access when you need it, and produces the documentation your building records require.

### 🔵 C-Bus Contract Inclusions

Scheduled maintenance visits using CBus Toolkit software, physical inspection of panels and units, scene and schedule verification, fault log review, written report, priority reactive callout and parts discount.

* Scheduled maintenance visits — annual, biannual or quarterly
* C-Bus network health diagnostic (CBus Toolkit / CGate)
* Physical panel and unit inspection — terminals, heat, connections
* Firmware check and update recommendations
* Scene, schedule and automation logic verification
* Priority reactive callout response between visits
* Written maintenance report after every visit
* Parts discount for contract clients on reactive work

FAQs

C-Bus Service Contract Sydney — Your Questions Answered
-------------------------------------------------------

What does a C-Bus service contract include? +

Our C-Bus service contracts include scheduled maintenance visits (your choice of annual, biannual or quarterly frequency), C-Bus network diagnostics using CBus Toolkit software, physical inspection, scene verification, written reports after every visit and priority reactive callout access.

How is the contract price set? +

Contract pricing is based on the size and complexity of your C-Bus installation, your required visit frequency and any additional inclusions. We assess the system and provide a fixed annual price — you'll know your maintenance budget before signing.

Can I get a C-Bus service contract for a strata building? +

Yes — strata common-area C-Bus systems are a core part of our contract maintenance work. We provide written reports after every visit formatted for strata body corporate records, and can coordinate with strata managers and building managers directly.

What happens if I need a reactive callout between scheduled visits? +

Contract clients receive priority access for reactive callouts — you go to the front of the scheduling queue. We also offer a discounted callout rate for reactive work carried out under a service contract.

Can a service contract cover both C-Bus and other systems? +

Yes — see our lighting control service contract page for multi-system agreements covering C-Bus, Dynalite, DALI and RAPIX under a single contract.
