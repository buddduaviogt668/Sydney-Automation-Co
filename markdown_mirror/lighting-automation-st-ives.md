FAQs

Lighting Automation St Ives — Your Questions Answered
-----------------------------------------------------

Can C-Bus control outdoor and garden lighting across my large St Ives property? +

Absolutely — outdoor lighting automation is a core C-Bus strength on St Ives's large blocks. We program dusk-to-dawn schedules, motion-triggered pathway lighting, entertainment area scenes and seasonal adjustments. C-Bus relay modules handle both low-voltage landscape lighting and mains-voltage floodlights in the same system.

Can you integrate my St Ives C-Bus system with my pool and home theatre? +

Yes. C-Bus integrates natively with most pool control systems, home theatre AV receivers and motorised blinds via relay outputs and third-party integration modules. We regularly set up Movie Night scenes that simultaneously dim the lights, close the blinds and trigger the AV system — all from a single keypad press or Siri command.

How long does a full C-Bus installation take in a large St Ives home? +

A full C-Bus installation in a large St Ives home typically takes 2–5 days depending on the number of zones, the complexity of integration required and whether infrastructure wiring is already in place. We provide a detailed project timeline and fixed-price quote before any work commences.

Do you send your own technicians to St Ives, or subcontractors? +

We send our own accredited technicians to all St Ives jobs — no subcontractors. The person who answers the phone is the same business that turns up at your door. All our technicians hold current Clipsal C-Bus accreditation.

Do you provide documentation after a St Ives job? +

Yes. Every job includes a written record of work carried out, faults identified and any recommendations. For strata and commercial clients we provide documentation suitable for body corporate and facilities management records.
