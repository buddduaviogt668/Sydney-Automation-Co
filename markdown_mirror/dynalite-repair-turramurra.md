FAQs

Dynalite Repair Turramurra — Your Questions Answered
----------------------------------------------------

What are the most common Dynalite faults in Turramurra? +

The most common Dynalite faults we repair in Turramurra include DDIM dimmer module failures (flickering or non-responsive lights), DPDMX processor faults, DUS universal station keypad failures, loss of Dynalite network communication, and programming corruption after a power surge or outage.

Is Dynalite repair different from C-Bus repair? +

Yes — Dynalite and C-Bus are completely different systems requiring different diagnostic software, hardware knowledge and accreditation. We hold current Philips Dynalite accreditation and use Dynalite Envision software for diagnostics and reprogramming. A general Automation Specialist cannot properly diagnose a Dynalite system.

Do you repair Dynalite in Turramurra strata and commercial buildings? +

Yes. We work with strata managers, body corporates and commercial building owners throughout Turramurra and the Upper North Shore. We provide written reports for strata records and DALI compliance certificates where required.

Do you send your own technicians to Turramurra, or subcontractors? +

We send our own accredited technicians to all Turramurra jobs — no subcontractors. The person who answers the phone is the same business that turns up at your door. All our technicians hold current Clipsal C-Bus accreditation.

Do you provide documentation after a Turramurra job? +

Yes. Every job includes a written record of work carried out, faults identified and any recommendations. For strata and commercial clients we provide documentation suitable for body corporate and facilities management records.
