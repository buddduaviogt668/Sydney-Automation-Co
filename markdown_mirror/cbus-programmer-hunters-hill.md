FAQs

C-Bus Programming Hunters Hill — Your Questions Answered
--------------------------------------------------------

What C-Bus programming do heritage Hunters Hill homes typically need? +

Heritage Hunters Hill properties often require programming that respects the architecture — keypad placement in original cornicing, scene names that match formal room functions, and outdoor lighting that highlights rather than overwhelms heritage facades. We work closely with owners and heritage architects to deliver programming that suits the home's character.

Can you integrate C-Bus with home security in a Hunters Hill property? +

Yes. C-Bus integrates with most major security systems via logic gate programming and dry contact inputs. Common integrations include: lights-on when security alarm triggers, Away mode that arms the security system and sets lighting schedules simultaneously, and exterior lighting triggered by CCTV motion zones.

How long does C-Bus programming take in a Hunters Hill home? +

Most residential C-Bus programming jobs in Hunters Hill take 3–6 hours depending on the number of zones and integration complexity. We quote a fixed scope before starting and provide full project documentation on completion.

Do you send your own technicians to Hunters Hill, or subcontractors? +

We send our own accredited technicians to all Hunters Hill jobs — no subcontractors. The person who answers the phone is the same business that turns up at your door. All our technicians hold current Clipsal C-Bus accreditation.

Do you provide documentation after a Hunters Hill job? +

Yes. Every job includes a written record of work carried out, faults identified and any recommendations. For strata and commercial clients we provide documentation suitable for body corporate and facilities management records.
