AFSS Compliance — Annual Fire Safety Statement
----------------------------------------------

Emergency lighting repairs that make your AFSS inspection a non-issue

#### What is AFSS?

An Annual Fire Safety Statement (AFSS) is a mandatory legal requirement in NSW. Your building must have all fire safety systems — including emergency lighting — operational and tested each year by a certified fire safety practitioner. If emergency lighting fails, your AFSS cannot be issued. We help you find and fix those faults before your inspection date.

🔧

Pre-Inspection Fault Diagnosis

Get your emergency lighting tested and faults identified before your formal AFSS inspection. We fix spitfires, exit signs, battens, RAPIX systems and C-Bus/Dynalite emergency controls.

### 📋 Our AFSS Compliance & Testing Process

We ensure your building meets all NSW Fire Safety Standards (AS 2293) with our rigorous testing protocol:

* 🔍 **6-Monthly Duration Tests:** Verifying battery backup for full 90 minutes.
* 🔍 **Exit Light Visibility Checks:** Ensuring clear visibility in smoke conditions.
* 🔍 **Battery Health Analysis:** Proactive replacement before failure occurs.
* 🔍 **Official AFSS Form Signing:** Providing documentation for your Annual Fire Safety Statement.

*Avoid hefty fines from Fire & Rescue NSW. Keep your Annual Fire Safety Statement (AFSS) up to date with our certified inspections.*
