Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------

Common questions about our services. Can't find your answer? [Contact us](/contact) or call [0422 469 739](tel:0422469739).

What is Dynalite lighting control?
+

Dynalite is a professional lighting control system by Philips/Signify used in commercial, hospitality, and high-end residential buildings across Sydney and Australia.

How much does Dynalite programming cost in Sydney?
+

Dynalite programming costs depend on system complexity. Contact Sydney Automation Co. on 0422 469 739 for a fixed-price quote.

Can you fix a Dynalite system that has stopped working?
+

Yes, we specialise in Dynalite fault finding and repairs across Sydney. We offer same-day callouts for urgent faults.

What is the difference between Dynalite and C-Bus?
+

C-Bus is made by Clipsal/Schneider Electric and is common in residential and commercial buildings. Dynalite is made by Philips/Signify and is more common in hospitality and large commercial projects. Both are professional lighting control systems.
