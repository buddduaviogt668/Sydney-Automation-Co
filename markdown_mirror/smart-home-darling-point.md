FAQs

Smart Home Installation Darling Point — Your Questions Answered
---------------------------------------------------------------

What's the best smart home system for a Darling Point harbourside apartment? +

For Darling Point apartments we typically recommend Clipsal C-Bus or Philips Dynalite as the lighting control backbone, integrated with Apple HomeKit or Control4 for whole-home automation including blinds, HVAC, AV and security. The right choice depends on existing infrastructure, strata requirements and your preferred interface.

Can you upgrade the lighting control in my existing Darling Point apartment? +

Yes — apartment upgrades are a specialty. We work within existing infrastructure, strata bylaws and building management requirements to deliver significant improvements: reprogrammed scenes, smartphone control, voice integration and automated schedules — without a full rewire.

Do you work with strata managers and building managers in Darling Point? +

Yes. We work directly with strata managers, body corporates and building managers on C-Bus and Dynalite maintenance, upgrades and fault repairs in Darling Point's apartment buildings. We provide strata-compliant documentation and work outside business hours where required to avoid disruption to residents.

Do you send your own technicians to Darling Point, or subcontractors? +

We send our own accredited technicians to all Darling Point jobs — no subcontractors. The person who answers the phone is the same business that turns up at your door. All our technicians hold current Clipsal C-Bus accreditation.

Do you provide documentation after a Darling Point job? +

Yes. Every job includes a written record of work carried out, faults identified and any recommendations. For strata and commercial clients we provide documentation suitable for body corporate and facilities management records.
