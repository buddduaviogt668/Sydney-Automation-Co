📱 Modernize & Voice Control

Don't let your C-Bus system feel dated. We integrate legacy systems with **Apple HomeKit, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa**. Control your lighting, blinds, and climate from your iPhone or Watch with remote access from anywhere.

🔧 Specialist Diagnostics

We fix the "unfixable." Using **C-Bus Toolkit, PICED, and Dynalite Envision**, we perform deep-level network analysis, burden testing, and voltage drop diagnostics that general Automation Specialists cannot provide.

🚨 Emergency Same-Day Repairs

Lights won't turn off? Total system crash? We offer **Priority Same-Day Service** across Sydney for critical faults. Our vans are stocked with common C-Bus & Dynalite hardware for immediate replacement.
