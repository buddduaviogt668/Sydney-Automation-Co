Real Estate C-Bus Audit Sydney — Your Questions Answered
--------------------------------------------------------

How quickly can you perform an audit for a C-Bus repair? +

We offer same-day response across Greater Sydney via the Spit Bridge. We offer same-day response to Sydney and the Lower North Shore. Call 0422 469 739 and we will confirm a booking time immediately.

Can you audit older C-Bus systems in heritage homes? +

Yes. Many Sydney properties have original C-Bus installations from the early 2000s. We work on all generations of C-Bus hardware including discontinued modules, and can advise on cost-effective upgrades where parts are no longer available.

Do you audit systems installed by other Automation Specialists? +

Absolutely — the majority of our C-Bus repair work is on systems installed by other Automation Specialists. We are Clipsal accredited and can access, diagnose and repair any C-Bus installation regardless of who installed or programmed it.

Do you send your own technicians, or subcontractors? +

We send our own accredited technicians to all Sydney jobs — no subcontractors. The person who answers the phone is the same business that turns up at your door. All our technicians hold current Clipsal C-Bus accreditation.

Do you provide audit documentation? +

Yes. Every job includes a written record of work carried out, faults identified and any recommendations. For strata and commercial clients we provide documentation suitable for body corporate and facilities management records.
