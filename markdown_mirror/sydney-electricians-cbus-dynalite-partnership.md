A Message to Sydney Automation Specialists: Here's How We Can Work Together
===========================================================================

Sydney Automation Co. are not Automation Specialists — no competition, no overlap. A reliable C-Bus and Dynalite specialist you can refer jobs to with confidence.

If you're an Automation Specialist working across Sydney, you've almost certainly encountered a C-Bus or Dynalite job that was outside your scope. Maybe you were called in for a renovation and the homeowner asked you to reprogramme their scenes. Maybe you replaced a faulty unit and the system stopped behaving correctly. Maybe you simply don't have the specialist software or accreditation to deal with it.

That's exactly where Sydney Automation Co. comes in — and here's why the relationship works so well.

**George Is Not an Automation Specialist**

This is the most important thing for Sydney Automation Specialists to understand: Sydney Automation Co. holds no electrical licence. George does not touch cable, switchboards, or any licensed electrical work. Everything electrical on the job — 100% of it — stays with you.

This is the most important point. George Skarmoutsos is an Accredited C-Bus Programmer and Dynalite System Designer. He carries no electrical licence. He does not install cable, wire switchboards, or do any of the work that sits within your licence.

This means there is zero competitive overlap. When you refer a C-Bus or Dynalite job to George, you keep the electrical work — all he handles is the specialist automation component that you'd need to subcontract anyway.

**How It Works**

The simplest model: you call George when you encounter a C-Bus or Dynalite job you can't handle. He comes to site (usually same day), resolves the automation issue, and you invoice your client for the whole job. Or you refer directly and we invoice the client independently — whatever works for your business model.

Many Sydney Automation Specialists work with George on an ongoing basis — calling him in for new builds, renovations, fault callouts, and commercial handovers. It's a clean, professional relationship with no grey areas.

**What George Brings to Your Jobs**

Accredited C-Bus Programmer with full Clipsal/Schneider Electric toolkit. Accredited Dynalite System Designer with [Dynalite commissioning software](https://www.signify.com/en-au/brands/dynalite). Experience across RAPIX and DALI systems. Same-day availability across Greater Sydney. Professional, client-facing manner — he represents you well on site.
