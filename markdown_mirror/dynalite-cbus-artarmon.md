FAQs

C-Bus & Dynalite Repair Artarmon — Your Questions Answered
----------------------------------------------------------

Do you repair commercial Dynalite systems in Artarmon office buildings? +

Yes — commercial Dynalite repair is a core service. We work in Artarmon's office buildings, including multi-tenancy commercial buildings where Dynalite controls common areas, individual tenancy zones and carpark lighting. We carry out after-hours repairs to avoid business disruption and provide DALI and Dynalite compliance certificates where required.

My Artarmon home has C-Bus and I'm not sure who installed it — can you still repair it? +

Absolutely. The majority of our repair work is on systems installed by other trades. We are Clipsal-accredited, use the official C-Bus Toolkit diagnostic software, and can access, assess and repair any C-Bus installation regardless of who originally installed or programmed it.

Can you service both C-Bus and Dynalite in the same Artarmon building? +

Yes. Many Artarmon commercial buildings have C-Bus in tenant fit-outs and Dynalite in common areas — or vice versa. We hold accreditation in both systems and can service a building with mixed lighting control infrastructure in a single visit, which is significantly more efficient than using two separate contractors.

Do you send your own technicians to Artarmon, or subcontractors? +

We send our own accredited technicians to all Artarmon jobs — no subcontractors. The person who answers the phone is the same business that turns up at your door. All our technicians hold current Clipsal C-Bus accreditation.

Do you provide documentation after a Artarmon job? +

Yes. Every job includes a written record of work carried out, faults identified and any recommendations. For strata and commercial clients we provide documentation suitable for body corporate and facilities management records.
