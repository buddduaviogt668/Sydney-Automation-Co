Dynalite Service Contract — What's Included
-------------------------------------------

Dynalite systems in commercial and strata buildings operate 24/7, controlling common areas, carparks and lobby lighting. A service contract ensures systematic maintenance, not just reactive repairs when something stops working.

### 🟠 Dynalite Contract Inclusions

Scheduled maintenance visits using DynaDesign/Envision software, DynaNet bus health checks, processor and module inspection, scene verification, fault log review, written report, priority reactive callout and parts discount.

* Scheduled maintenance visits — annual, biannual or quarterly
* DynaNet bus diagnostics via DynaDesign / Envision software
* Processor, module and driver physical inspection
* Scene preset, zone and automation logic verification
* Sensor and occupancy calibration check
* Priority reactive callout response between visits
* Written maintenance report after every visit
* Parts discount for contract clients on reactive work

FAQs

Dynalite Service Contract Sydney — Your Questions Answered
----------------------------------------------------------

What does a Dynalite service contract include? +

Our Dynalite service contracts include scheduled maintenance visits (annual, biannual or quarterly), DynaNet bus diagnostics using DynaDesign/Envision software, physical inspection, scene verification, priority reactive callout access and written reports after every visit.

How is Dynalite contract pricing determined? +

Pricing is based on the complexity of your Dynalite installation, number of devices, visit frequency and any additional inclusions. We assess the system and provide a fixed annual price before any agreement is signed.

Do you service Dynalite in strata common areas under a contract? +

Yes — strata common-area Dynalite systems are a core part of our contract work. Written reports are formatted for body corporate records, and we can coordinate with strata managers and building managers directly.

What if there's a fault between scheduled visits? +

Contract clients have priority reactive access. When a fault occurs between scheduled visits, you go to the front of the queue. Reactive work carried out under a service contract is also provided at a discounted rate.

Can a Dynalite contract include DALI maintenance? +

Yes — many Dynalite systems in commercial buildings control DALI-addressed drivers. We can include DALI bus maintenance within a Dynalite service contract, or you can opt for a multi-system contract covering all your lighting control systems.
