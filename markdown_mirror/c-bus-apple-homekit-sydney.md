How It Works

Your C-Bus System, Apple Home Ready.
------------------------------------

C-Bus and Apple HomeKit can work together through a HomeKit bridge — software that translates between the C-Bus network and Apple's HomeKit protocol. This lets your iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Siri control C-Bus lights, scenes and groups without replacing any existing hardware.

The most common approach uses the C-Bus Wiser for HomeKit gateway or third-party bridge solutions like Homebridge running on a local server. We assess your system and recommend the right approach for your setup.

* Control C-Bus lights via Apple Home app
* Use Siri voice control for lighting scenes
* Set up HomeKit automations triggered by time, location or sensors
* Remote access when away from home via Apple TV or HomePod hub
* Works with existing C-Bus hardware — no wiring changes needed

C-Bus HomeKit FAQs
------------------

Do I need to replace my C-Bus hardware to use Apple HomeKit? +

No. The integration is done via a bridge or gateway device that connects your existing C-Bus network to HomeKit. Your C-Bus switches, relay modules and dimmer units all stay in place.

Will the C-Bus switches still work normally after HomeKit integration? +

Yes — HomeKit integration is additive. Your existing C-Bus wall switches and keypads continue to work exactly as before. HomeKit simply adds another way to control the same lighting groups.

Can I control C-Bus from outside my home with HomeKit? +

Yes, with an Apple TV (4th gen or later), HomePod or iPad set up as a home hub in your home. This acts as a relay so you can control your C-Bus lighting from anywhere via the Apple Home app.

What C-Bus hardware do I need for HomeKit integration? +

You'll need either a C-Bus network interface (CNI) connected to your local network, or a Wiser for C-Bus unit already installed. Most C-Bus systems installed in the last 15 years have one of these. We'll confirm during the assessment.

Can you integrate C-Bus with Google Home or Amazon Alexa as well? +

Yes — if you'd like Google Home or Alexa control in addition to or instead of HomeKit, we can set that up as well. Ask us about multi-platform integration when you get in touch.
