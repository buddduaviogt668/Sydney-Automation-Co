How to Choose a C-Bus Specialist
--------------------------------

Not everyone who claims to work with C-Bus is a genuine specialist. Here's what to look for.

1

### Check for Clipsal Accreditation

The Clipsal / Schneider Electric Accredited C-Bus Programmer credential is the only formally recognised qualification for C-Bus programming in Australia. Ask for it. If they can't provide it, they're not a specialist.

2

### Confirm They Have C-Bus Toolkit

C-Bus Toolkit is the essential software for network-level diagnosis, programming and commissioning. Without it, a technician is working blind — they can replace hardware but they can't see the network or fix software faults.

3

### Ask About Their C-Bus Experience

How many C-Bus systems have they worked on? Do they work on residential and commercial? Have they dealt with fault finding, not just new installs? Generic automation experience doesn't equal C-Bus specialist experience.

4

### Look for Fixed Pricing

A genuine C-Bus specialist can give you a fixed price for defined scope — programming sessions, fault finding callouts, commissioning. Vague hourly rates with no ceiling often lead to bill shock.
