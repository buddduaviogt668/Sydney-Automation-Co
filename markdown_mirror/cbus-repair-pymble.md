FAQs

C-Bus Repair Pymble — Your Questions Answered
---------------------------------------------

My Pymble C-Bus keypads went dark after a power outage — what's happened? +

A power surge or extended outage can cause C-Bus PCIA units, power supply modules or individual dimmer units to fail. In some cases the C-Bus project file can become corrupted. We carry portable diagnostic tools to assess the full extent of damage on the first visit and restore your system.

Do you repair C-Bus outdoor garden lighting in Pymble? +

Yes — garden and landscape lighting controlled by C-Bus is common in Pymble's larger properties. We repair relay modules, C-Bus input units used for motion and dusk-dawn triggers, and reprogram outdoor lighting schedules. We also identify wiring damage from landscaping work, which is a frequent cause of outdoor C-Bus faults.

How long will a C-Bus repair take in my Pymble home? +

Most C-Bus repairs in Pymble are completed in 1–3 hours. Simple faults like a failed dimmer module, keypad replacement or power supply are typically diagnosed and resolved in a single visit. Complex network faults or post-renovation damage may require additional time, which we scope clearly before proceeding.

Do you send your own technicians to Pymble, or subcontractors? +

We send our own accredited technicians to all Pymble jobs — no subcontractors. The person who answers the phone is the same business that turns up at your door. All our technicians hold current Clipsal C-Bus accreditation.

Do you provide documentation after a Pymble job? +

Yes. Every job includes a written record of work carried out, faults identified and any recommendations. For strata and commercial clients we provide documentation suitable for body corporate and facilities management records.
