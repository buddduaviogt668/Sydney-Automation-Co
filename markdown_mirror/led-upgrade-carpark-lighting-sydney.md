Why Upgrade to LED?

The Business Case for LED Car Park Lighting

LED Upgrade FAQ

Common Questions About LED Upgrades
-----------------------------------

Why should I upgrade my car park lighting to LED? +

LED car park lighting delivers significant energy savings (typically 50-70% reduction in energy consumption), dramatically reduced maintenance costs due to longer lamp life, improved light quality and uniformity, and compliance with current Australian Standards for car park lighting. Modern LED fittings also integrate emergency lighting functionality, simplifying compliance.

Can you upgrade the emergency lighting in our car park at the same time? +

Yes. We recommend combining emergency lighting upgrades with general LED upgrades wherever possible. Modern LED emergency battens and exit signs can be installed at the same time as the general lighting upgrade, minimising disruption and reducing overall project cost.

Do you have relationships with lighting manufacturers? +

Yes. Sydney Automation Co. has established relationships with leading lighting manufacturers. This allows us to source quality LED products at competitive prices and to access technical support and product warranties that protect your investment.

How long does a car park LED upgrade take? +

Duration depends on the size of the car park and the number of fittings to be replaced. A typical strata car park upgrade can be completed in one to two days. Larger shopping centre or commercial car parks are typically staged over multiple nights or weekends to minimise disruption.

Will the LED upgrade help us pass our AFSS? +

Yes. When we upgrade your car park lighting, we include the emergency lighting component — replacing aging emergency battens and exit signs with compliant LED products. We provide full test records and compliance documentation as part of the upgrade, supporting your AFSS certification.

Can you integrate the LED lighting with our building management system? +

Yes. We can specify DALI-compatible LED products that integrate with building management systems for centralised control and energy monitoring. We are DALI specialists and can design and commission DALI lighting control systems alongside LED upgrades.
