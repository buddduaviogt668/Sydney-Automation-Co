We also provide compliance documentation and as-built records. Do you handle emergency lighting compliance in carparks? + Yes — emergency lighting compliance is an integral part of every carpark upgrade we scope. We replace non-compliant emergency battens and exit signs, commission the emergency lighting circuit and provide AFSS-related testing records where required. Can you upgrade carpark lighting in a strata building? + Yes. We work with strata managers and body corporates on carpark upgrades regularly. We provide written scopes for committee approval, stage works to minimise access disruption and deliver documentation in formats suitable for strata records. Do you integrate occupancy sensors in carpark upgrades? + Yes — occupancy sensing is a standard inclusion in our carpark upgrade scopes. Motion sensors in each bay or zone reduce operating hours of the LED system, maximising energy savings. We commission and calibrate sensors as part of the project. Can you upgrade just the emergency lighting in our carpark? + Yes — emergency lighting upgrades can be scoped and delivered independently of general LED upgrades. If your general lighting is already LED but the emergency lighting is failing or non-compliant, we can address just the emergency system.
