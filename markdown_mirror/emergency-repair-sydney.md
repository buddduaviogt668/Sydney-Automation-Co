Who Calls Us

Emergency Clients We Serve Every Week
-------------------------------------

Lighting control emergencies happen to all building types. We respond to urgent calls from:

* **Strata managers** — lobby, car park and common area failures
* **Hotel operators** — guest floor and function room faults
* **Facilities managers** — commercial office floor failures
* **Homeowners** — whole-home C-Bus or Dynalite system down
* **Automation Specialists** — who need a specialist to take over the fault
* **Property managers** — tenant complaints about lighting faults
* **Builders** — commissioning failures on project handover
