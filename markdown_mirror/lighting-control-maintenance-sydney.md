----------------------------------------------------------

Many Sydney buildings run more than one lighting control system — C-Bus in apartments, Dynalite in common areas, DALI drivers throughout. Most contractors service one. We service all four under a single maintenance agreement.

* RAPIX controller and device health check
* Scene, schedule and automation logic review across all systems
* Fault log review and preventative part identification
* Emergency lighting integration check where applicable
* Written FM-ready report after every visit

FAQs

Lighting Control Maintenance — Your Questions Answered
------------------------------------------------------

What lighting control systems do you maintain? +

We maintain all major lighting control systems used in Sydney buildings: Clipsal C-Bus, Philips Dynalite, DALI and DALI-2, and RAPIX (Veroboard) systems. Most Sydney commercial buildings run a combination of these, and we cover them all under a single maintenance agreement.

Can one maintenance agreement cover multiple systems? +

Yes — and it's one of our key advantages. We offer multi-system maintenance contracts covering C-Bus, Dynalite, DALI and RAPIX in a single agreement. You get one point of contact, one scheduled visit and one consolidated report.

Do you maintain systems you haven't installed? +

Yes. The majority of our maintenance clients have systems installed by others. We audit, document and maintain any correctly installed C-Bus, Dynalite, DALI or RAPIX system regardless of who installed it.

How often should lighting control systems be serviced? +

Commercial building systems benefit from biannual maintenance in most cases. Buildings with higher complexity, more tenants or compliance obligations (AFSS, emergency lighting) may warrant quarterly visits. We recommend a schedule after an initial site assessment.

Do you provide documentation for strata and FM records? +

Yes. Every maintenance visit generates a written report documenting system health, faults found, actions taken and recommendations. These reports are formatted for strata body corporate records and facility management reporting.
