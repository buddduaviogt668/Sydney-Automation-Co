C-Bus Repair Edgecliff — Your Questions Answered
------------------------------------------------

Can you repair C-Bus in a high-rise Edgecliff apartment building? +

Yes — high-rise apartment C-Bus repair is well within our scope. We work with building managers, strata agents and individual owners in Edgecliff high-rise buildings. Access requirements are coordinated with building management prior to each visit.

My Edgecliff apartment has C-Bus but I've lost the documentation — can you still repair it? +

Yes. We use C-Bus Toolkit software to connect directly to your C-Bus network and extract a full system map — no documentation required. We then provide you with a complete project backup and documentation of your system as it stands, which is valuable for future maintenance.

Can you service both C-Bus apartments and Dynalite common areas in my Edgecliff building? +

Yes — this is one of our specialties. Many Edgecliff buildings have C-Bus in apartments and Dynalite (or DALI) in common areas and carparks. We hold dual accreditation and can carry out all lighting control work across a building in a single visit, with full documentation provided to the building manager on completion.

Do you send your own technicians to Edgecliff, or subcontractors? +

We send our own accredited technicians to all Edgecliff jobs — no subcontractors. The person who answers the phone is the same business that turns up at your door. All our technicians hold current Clipsal C-Bus accreditation.

Do you provide documentation after a Edgecliff job? +

Yes. Every job includes a written record of work carried out, faults identified and any recommendations. For strata and commercial clients we provide documentation suitable for body corporate and facilities management records.
