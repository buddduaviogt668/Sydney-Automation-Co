FAQs

Lighting Control Rose Bay — Your Questions Answered
---------------------------------------------------

What's the difference between C-Bus and Dynalite for a Rose Bay home? +

Both are professional lighting control systems, but they suit different applications. C-Bus is typically the better choice for large residential homes where integration with smart home platforms, security and HVAC is required. Dynalite is often found in high-end apartment buildings and commercial fit-outs. We can advise on which is installed in your Rose Bay property and what's possible with your existing infrastructure.

Can you upgrade my Rose Bay home from standard dimmers to full C-Bus automation? +

Yes — this is a common project in Rose Bay. If your home has existing standard dimmers, we assess whether upgrading to C-Bus is viable within your current wiring infrastructure. In many cases a C-Bus retrofit is more straightforward than owners expect, particularly in homes built after 1995. We provide a free site assessment and written scope before any work is quoted.

Do you repair C-Bus and Dynalite in Rose Bay apartment buildings? +

Yes. We work with strata managers, body corporates and individual owners in Rose Bay's apartment buildings on both C-Bus residential repairs and Dynalite common area maintenance. We provide strata-compliant documentation and work to strata building access requirements.

Do you send your own technicians to Rose Bay, or subcontractors? +

We send our own accredited technicians to all Rose Bay jobs — no subcontractors. The person who answers the phone is the same business that turns up at your door. All our technicians hold current Clipsal C-Bus accreditation.

Do you provide documentation after a Rose Bay job? +

Yes. Every job includes a written record of work carried out, faults identified and any recommendations. For strata and commercial clients we provide documentation suitable for body corporate and facilities management records.
