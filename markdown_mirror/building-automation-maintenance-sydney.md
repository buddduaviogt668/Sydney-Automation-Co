Lighting Control in Commercial Building Automation
--------------------------------------------------

Modern commercial buildings integrate multiple systems — lighting control, HVAC, access control and BMS — into a single automation layer. Our maintenance specialisation is the lighting control component: C-Bus, Dynalite, DALI and RAPIX, including how they interface with the BMS.

### 🏢 Building Automation Maintenance Scope

C-Bus and Dynalite system health, DALI driver maintenance, RAPIX network checks, BMS-to-lighting interface verification, occupancy and daylight harvesting calibration, and written FM-ready reports.

* C-Bus and Dynalite network health diagnostics
* DALI and DALI-2 bus maintenance and driver health
* RAPIX controller and device network checks
* BMS-to-lighting control interface verification
* Occupancy sensing and daylight harvesting calibration
* Schedule and automation logic review across all systems
* Emergency lighting integration check
* Written FM-ready report after every maintenance visit

FAQs

Building Automation Maintenance — Your Questions Answered
---------------------------------------------------------

What building automation systems do you maintain? +

We specialise in the lighting control layer of building automation: Clipsal C-Bus, Philips Dynalite, DALI and DALI-2, and RAPIX. We also maintain the interface between these systems and building management systems (BMS).

Can you maintain lighting control systems that are integrated with a BMS? +

Yes — BMS integration is a specific area of our expertise. We verify that C-Bus and Dynalite lighting control systems communicate correctly with the BMS, including occupancy overrides, time-based scheduling and emergency lighting scenarios.

Do you maintain building automation systems beyond lighting? +

Our primary specialisation is lighting control — C-Bus, Dynalite, DALI and RAPIX. We don't maintain HVAC or access control systems directly, but we understand how they interface with the lighting control layer and can work alongside BMS contractors.

What documentation do you provide for commercial building managers? +

After every maintenance visit, we provide a written report documenting system health, faults found, actions taken and recommendations. We also provide annual health summaries suitable for capital works planning and FM reporting.

Can you set up a service contract for building automation maintenance? +

Yes — see our lighting control service contract page. Contracts can be structured to cover all lighting control systems in your building under a single fixed-price annual agreement with priority reactive callout access.
