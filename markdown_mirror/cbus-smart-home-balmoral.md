FAQs

C-Bus Smart Home Balmoral — Your Questions Answered
---------------------------------------------------

Can you add smartphone control to my existing Balmoral C-Bus system? +

Yes — this is one of the most requested upgrades in Balmoral and Mosman. We integrate your existing C-Bus installation with Apple HomeKit via a hardware bridge, giving you full control from your iPhone and Apple Watch, including remote access when you're away. No replacement of existing hardware required in most cases.

My Balmoral property has a guest house with its own C-Bus zone — can you connect both buildings? +

Yes — multi-building C-Bus networks are common in larger Balmoral properties. We connect your main house and guest house (or pool house, garage etc.) into a single C-Bus network using C-Bus CNI units, allowing both buildings to share scenes, schedules and remote control from a single app.

Does salt air damage C-Bus systems in Balmoral waterfront homes? +

Yes — this is a real issue in harbourside properties. Salt-laden air accelerates corrosion in outdoor C-Bus wiring terminations, relay modules and sensor units. We identify and rectify corrosion damage during repairs and can advise on better enclosure specifications for outdoor hardware in exposed locations.

Do you send your own technicians to Balmoral, or subcontractors? +

We send our own accredited technicians to all Balmoral jobs — no subcontractors. The person who answers the phone is the same business that turns up at your door. All our technicians hold current Clipsal C-Bus accreditation.

Do you provide documentation after a Balmoral job? +

Yes. Every job includes a written record of work carried out, faults identified and any recommendations. For strata and commercial clients we provide documentation suitable for body corporate and facilities management records.
