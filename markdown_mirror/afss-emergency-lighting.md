What Causes Emergency Lighting to Fail Your AFSS?
-------------------------------------------------

Emergency lighting must pass a 90-minute battery test and full functional test during your AFSS inspection. These are the faults we fix most often.

💡

Spitfire Lights Not Working

Corroded battery contacts, blown lamp, failed driver, or wiring faults. We diagnose and fix in one visit — no guessing, no return call-backs.

🚪

Exit Signs Dark or Dimming

Ballast failure, LED module burnout, or mains supply issues. We repair or replace and re-test to confirm compliance.

⚡

Battery Backup Not Holding Charge

Batteries degrade after 3–5 years. We test each fitting's runtime and replace packs that can't sustain the mandatory 90 minutes.

🔌

C-Bus / Dynalite Integration Faults

Emergency lighting wired into your C-Bus or Dynalite system not switching correctly? We reprogram and restore for full AFSS compliance.

🔦

Emergency Battens Not Activating

Failed central battery, inverter fault, or individual fitting issues. We trace the fault and restore activation within the required response time.

📋

Multiple Units Failing

When many fittings fail at once it often points to a shared power or control fault. We find the root cause — not just the symptom — and fix it properly.

Common Questions About AFSS Emergency Lighting
----------------------------------------------

### What is an AFSS inspection?

An Annual Fire Safety Statement (AFSS) is a mandatory document that NSW building owners must submit to their local council each year. It confirms all essential fire safety measures — including emergency lighting — are working and maintained to the required standard. Failing to submit, or submitting with non-compliant systems, can result in council fines and legal exposure.

### How quickly can you fix our emergency lighting?

In most cases we can attend your site and complete repairs within 48 hours. For urgent AFSS deadlines — especially if your inspection is days away — call us immediately on [0422 469 739](tel:0422469739) and we'll fast-track your repair around your schedule.
