Scheduled Dynalite Maintenance Sydney Buildings
-----------------------------------------------

Dynalite systems in commercial and strata buildings work hardest when most people aren't watching — controlling common areas, carparks and lobby lighting 24/7. Scheduled maintenance keeps them compliant, reliable and invisible.

### 🔧 What Dynalite Maintenance Covers

Full Dynet protocol diagnostics, DynaDesign software review, processor and module inspection, scene and preset verification, fault logging and a written report after every visit.

* Dynalite network diagnostics via DynaDesign / Envision software
* DynaNet bus health check — voltage, topology, device addressing
* Processor, module and driver physical inspection
* Scene preset and zone logic verification
* Sensor, occupancy and daylight harvesting calibration check
* DALI driver health review where Dynalite controls DALI outputs
* Written maintenance report suitable for strata/FM records
* Priority callout response between scheduled visits

FAQs

Dynalite Maintenance Sydney — Your Questions Answered
-----------------------------------------------------

How often does a Dynalite system need servicing? +

Commercial Dynalite systems in high-traffic buildings typically benefit from quarterly or biannual maintenance. Strata common-area systems with lower-complexity programming may be suitable for annual servicing. We recommend a schedule after an initial site assessment.

Can you maintain a Dynalite system installed by someone else? +

Yes. Most of our Dynalite maintenance clients have systems installed by others. We use Philips DynaDesign and Envision software to interrogate the existing programming, document the installation and establish a baseline for ongoing servicing.

What's included in a Dynalite maintenance visit? +

A standard visit covers DynaNet bus diagnostics, physical inspection of processors and modules, scene and zone verification, sensor calibration check, fault log review and a written report. We carry common replacement parts and fix issues found on the day where possible.

Do you maintain Dynalite systems that also control DALI outputs? +

Yes — many commercial buildings use Dynalite as the control layer over DALI-addressed drivers. Our maintenance includes checking the Dynalite-to-DALI interface, driver addressing and DALI bus health.

Can you service Dynalite in strata buildings? +

Yes — strata common areas are a core part of our Dynalite maintenance work. We provide written reports after every visit suitable for body corporate records, and can coordinate with strata managers and building managers directly.
