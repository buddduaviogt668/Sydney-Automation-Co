FAQs

Facilities Lighting Maintenance —
Your Questions Answered

What lighting systems do you maintain for facilities managers? +
We maintain all major lighting control systems in commercial and strata buildings: Clipsal C-Bus, Philips Dynalite, DALI and DALI-2, RAPIX, and emergency lighting systems. Most facilities have a combination of these, and we cover all of them under a single maintenance agreement.
Do you provide documentation formatted for FM reporting? +
Yes — this is a priority for us. Written reports after every visit are formatted for FM reporting systems, building manager files and body corporate records. We can adapt report formats to match your existing FM documentation structure.
Can you coordinate access with building managers and strata committees? +
Yes — we regularly work within the access management processes of commercial buildings and strata complexes. We coordinate with building managers and facilities managers on scheduling, access requirements and tenant communications where needed.
Do you handle emergency lighting maintenance for facilities? +
Yes — emergency lighting maintenance (monthly flick test reporting, annual discharge testing and AFSS-related compliance documentation) can be included within our facilities maintenance scope. We provide records in formats suitable for AFSS annual fire safety statement submissions.
Can you maintain lighting systems across a portfolio of buildings? +
Yes — we work with facilities managers and strata managers responsible for multiple buildings. Portfolio maintenance agreements can be structured to cover multiple properties with consolidated reporting and a single annual invoice.
