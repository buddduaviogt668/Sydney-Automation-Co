What Is RAPIX

RAPIX — The Commercial
Lighting Standard

RAPIX is a networked lighting control platform increasingly specified in mid-to-large Sydney commercial buildings. It offers tight integration with DALI-2, BACnet and building management systems, making it a leading choice for modern commercial fit-outs.

As more Sydney buildings adopt RAPIX, specialist knowledge becomes critical — both for initial commissioning and for fault finding when something goes wrong.

Typical RAPIX Installations

  • Commercial office towers and multi-tenancy fit-outs
  • Hotels and large hospitality venues
  • Shopping centres and retail precincts
  • Universities and education campuses
  • Healthcare and aged care facilities
  • Industrial and logistics facilities
