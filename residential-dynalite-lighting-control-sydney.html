PHILIPS DYNALITE IN SYDNEY HOMES

Your Home Has Dynalite —
Now What?

Dynalite (now branded Signify Dynalite, formerly Philips Dynalite) is widely installed in Sydney prestige homes from the 2000s onward. Common in properties across Mosman, Vaucluse, Double Bay, Kirribilli, and the Upper North Shore.

The challenge: Dynalite requires specialist software (Dynalite System Builder) and accredited knowledge to program, repair, or expand. General electricians cannot access or modify the system. George is one of the very few accredited Signify Dynalite System Designers in Sydney who actively services residential installations.

Common Residential Dynalite Systems:

  • PDEG processor controlling multiple zones
  • DUS30CS dimmers for smooth scene control
  • Wall stations and keypads for room scene selection
  • Integrated motor control for blinds and curtains
  • Time-based schedules (morning, evening, away, entertain)
  • DALI-2 integration for modern LED control

BRAND CLARIFICATION

Dynalite was acquired by Philips Lighting, which rebranded as Signify in 2018. If you’re searching for “Philips Dynalite repair Sydney” or “Philips Dynalite programmer Sydney” — that’s exactly what we do. Same hardware, same DyNet bus protocol, same Dynalite System Builder software.

George is accredited directly by Signify as a Dynalite System Designer. If the system was installed under the Philips brand or the Signify brand, it makes no difference to the programming or repair process.

FAQ

Residential Dynalite Questions Answered

Do you repair Philips Dynalite in Sydney homes? +
Yes. George is an Accredited Signify Dynalite System Designer and services residential Dynalite (previously branded Philips Dynalite) across Sydney's North Shore, Eastern Suburbs, and Sutherland Shire. Call 0422 469 739.
My home Dynalite scenes have all been lost after a power outage — what happened? +
Power surges can corrupt the PDEG processor's scene database. George reconnects via Dynalite System Builder and restores or reprograms the scenes — usually resolved in a single visit.
Can residential Dynalite be programmed remotely? +
Yes, where the PDEG or DDNG processor has an Ethernet connection. George programs systems remotely across Sydney and Australia-wide including Gold Coast, Brisbane, and New Zealand.
How is Philips Dynalite different from Signify Dynalite? +
They are the same product. Philips Lighting was rebranded as Signify in 2018. The hardware, software (Dynalite System Builder), and DyNet bus protocol are unchanged. George is accredited by Signify.
