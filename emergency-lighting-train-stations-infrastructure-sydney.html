Why Infrastructure Is Different

Train Stations Need
Specialist Knowledge

Emergency lighting in public transport infrastructure, government buildings, and large-scale facilities is a different proposition to a standard commercial building. The systems are more complex, the environments are more demanding, and the compliance requirements are more stringent.

Train stations, in particular, present unique challenges — high-vibration environments, outdoor platforms exposed to weather, underground concourses with limited natural light, and 24/7 operational requirements that make maintenance windows difficult to schedule.

We have the experience, the accreditations, and the systems knowledge to work in these environments. RAPIX Emergency is widely deployed across NSW transport infrastructure — and we are RAPIX certified specialists.

RAPIX Emergency in Infrastructure

Why RAPIX Is the
Infrastructure Standard

RAPIX Emergency is a networked emergency lighting control and monitoring system that is increasingly specified for large commercial buildings, public infrastructure, and transport facilities across Australia. It provides centralised monitoring, automated testing, and detailed reporting — making it the preferred choice for facilities managers and compliance teams.

In train stations and large infrastructure, RAPIX Emergency allows facilities teams to monitor the status of hundreds or thousands of emergency lights from a single interface — and to generate the compliance reports required for AFSS and other certifications automatically.

When a RAPIX Emergency system develops a fault — whether it's a network communication error, a failed device, or a configuration issue — you need a certified specialist to diagnose and resolve it. That's us.
