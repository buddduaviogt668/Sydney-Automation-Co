FAQs

Building Lighting Upgrades Sydney —
Your Questions Answered

What types of building lighting upgrades do you manage? +
We manage LED retrofit programs, C-Bus system additions and expansions, Dynalite common area upgrades, DALI-2 compliance upgrades, emergency lighting integration and occupancy/daylight harvesting additions across commercial and strata buildings.
Do you handle the lighting control system or just the fittings? +
We specialise in the lighting control layer — C-Bus, Dynalite, DALI — which is what determines how the building lighting actually behaves. We can also manage fitting supply and installation in conjunction with the control upgrade.
Can you upgrade DALI systems to DALI-2 compliance? +
Yes. DALI-2 compliance under AS 62386 is increasingly required in NSW commercial buildings. We assess existing DALI installations, identify non-compliant drivers and controllers, and manage the upgrade to DALI-2 standard with appropriate documentation.
Do you work with strata managers on building lighting upgrades? +
Yes — strata managers are a primary client group for our upgrade services. We provide written scopes, fixed quotes and staged works programs that minimise disruption to residents and common areas. Documentation is provided in formats suitable for strata committee approval.
Can you integrate emergency lighting with general lighting upgrades? +
Yes. We recommend integrating emergency lighting upgrades with general lighting upgrades wherever possible — it reduces cost, minimises disruption and results in a more coherent system. We hold AFSS-related competencies for emergency lighting compliance in NSW.
