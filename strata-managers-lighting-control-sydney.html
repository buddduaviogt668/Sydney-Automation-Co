Strata Managers: Who to Call When Your Building\u2019s Lighting Control System Fails

The lobby lights are stuck on full and the electrician says it’s a “computer thing”. Here’s who to call and what to expect.

You have a lighting control fault in a managed building. The after-hours electrician has been on site, cannot fix it, and the residents are complaining. This is one of the most common scenarios George encounters across Sydney strata buildings \u2014 and it\u2019s almost always avoidable with the right specialist on speed dial.

Why a General Electrician Cannot Fix a Dynalite or C-Bus Fault

Lighting control systems like Dynalite and C-Bus are specialist platforms. They have their own proprietary software, network addressing protocols, and commissioning tools. A general electrician \u2014 even a good one \u2014 cannot diagnose a Dynalite RS485 network dropout or recover lost C-Bus scene programming without the right software and accreditation. It is not a reflection on their skills; it is simply outside their scope.

George is an Accredited C-Bus Programmer and Accredited Dynalite System Designer. He carries the official Clipsal C-Bus Toolkit, C-Bus Commission Spacelogic, and Dynalite commissioning software on every site visit. These are the only tools that can properly diagnose and repair faults on these systems.

The Most Common Strata Lighting Control Faults

Across Sydney strata buildings \u2014 from Eastern Suburbs apartments to North Shore residential towers and CBD mixed-use developments \u2014 the faults George sees most often are: controller dropouts on Dynalite RS485 networks, usually triggered by power events; C-Bus address conflicts introduced by electricians doing switchboard or tenancy work; scene programming loss after power failures; and emergency lighting non-compliance with NCC 2022 DALI-2 requirements.
