The Brief: A Tight Deadline in the Heart of the CBD

Sydney Automation Co. was engaged by the onsite electrical contractor to deliver a lighting control project at Tank Stream Labs — the co-working and innovation hub located at 333 Kent Street, Sydney, in the heart of the CBD.

The defining constraint was time. Practical completion and client handover were scheduled for the following day. In commercial delivery, handover dates are contractual — slipping them has knock-on consequences for the contractor, the building manager and the tenant. The job needed a fast, reliable, specialist response under pressure.

This is exactly the scenario where a general electrician or an integrator without on-hand Dynalite software and commissioning experience falls short. The project required rapid, accurate reprogramming of a live Signify Dynalite lighting control system with zero room for error.

The Delivery: Fast, Accurate Reprogramming & Commissioning

Our team responded quickly, interfacing directly with the Dynalite network and implementing the required changes to the lighting control system. The scope covered reprogramming the relevant scenes, groups and control configuration to meet the client's requirements — then testing everything to confirm full functionality.

Working in an occupied commercial building in the CBD brings its own challenges — coordination with building management, working around live tenancies, and delivering a flawless result under time pressure. The system was left fully operational, tested and ready for the scheduled practical completion and handover.

Result

The Tank Stream Labs lighting control system was fully reprogrammed, commissioned and handed over operational — meeting the tight practical completion deadline. Another successful project delivered on time, demonstrating Sydney Automation Co.'s commitment to efficient, dependable lighting control and automation for commercial clients across Sydney.
