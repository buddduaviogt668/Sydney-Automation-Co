Common Questions in Coogee

My Dynalite keypad in Coogee is unresponsive after a power surge — what should I do?

Power surges can corrupt Dynalite DDBC controller memory. Do not attempt a factory reset without backing up the configuration first. Call us and we will diagnose via the DyNet bus before attempting any reset.

How long does a Dynalite repair take in Coogee?

Standard keypad and scene repairs take 1-3 hours. Full system reprogramming for larger Coogee apartment buildings can take a full day depending on complexity.

Can you upgrade old C-Bus in a Coogee home to work with Apple HomeKit?

Yes — we install and configure the C-Bus to HomeKit bridge (via a SHAC or Wiser for KNX gateway), allowing your existing C-Bus hardware to be controlled via Siri and the Home app.
