Recommended C-Bus Replacement Parts

The most commonly failed C-Bus modules — stocked in our van and available for immediate supply and installation across Sydney.

⚡
SpaceLogic C-Bus

12-Channel Relay Module

PART NO: L5512RVF / L5516RVF

The standard DIN-rail replacement for sticking or buzzing relays. Resolves lights stuck on or off, intermittent channel failures, and relay chatter caused by age or overload.

View in Products Catalogue →
🔋
SpaceLogic C-Bus

System Power Supply

PART NO: 5500PS / 5500PSU

Replaces failed internal power sources causing complete network blackout. When bus voltage drops below 15V, keypads go dark and all units lose communication. The 5500PS restores full network power.

View in Products Catalogue →
🌐
SpaceLogic C-Bus

Network Interface (PCI)

PART NO: 5500PC / 5500PCU

Required when home automation servers, schedulers, or C-Bus Toolkit software can no longer connect to the network. Also resolves clock conflicts causing whole-network freezes.
