# Business Plan 2026: Sydney Automation Co.

## 1. Executive Summary
**Sydney Automation Co.** is a premier provider of specialized lighting control and building automation services across Greater Sydney. Led by **George Skarmoutsos**, an accredited C-Bus and Dynalite specialist, the company fills a critical gap in the market for high-level technical expertise in legacy and modern automation systems. We specialize in the "hard" technical aspects of automation: programming, fault finding, and complex system integration for both high-end residential and commercial sectors.

## 2. Market Opportunity
The Australian smart home market is projected to reach over **USD 15 billion by 2030**, with a strong focus on energy efficiency and seamless integration. Sydney, as Australia's most expensive property market, has a high density of legacy **C-Bus** and **Dynalite** installations that now require expert maintenance, upgrades, and integration with modern ecosystems like Apple HomeKit.

### Target Client Segments
| Segment | Ideal Client Profile | Pain Points |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High-End Builders** | Luxury residential builders in Eastern Suburbs, North Shore, and Northern Beaches. | Unreliable electrical contractors who "dabble" in automation but can't finish commissioning. |
| **Architects & Designers** | Boutique firms specializing in high-end residential and commercial fit-outs. | Technical limitations in realizing creative lighting designs; need for reliable technical partners. |
| **Strata & Facility Managers** | Managers of commercial towers and luxury apartment complexes. | High energy costs; aging lighting control systems causing tenant complaints. |
| **Homeowners** | Owners of luxury properties with existing C-Bus/Dynalite systems. | Systems that "don't work" or feel outdated; lack of mobile app control. |

## 3. Core Services & Specializations
Sydney Automation Co. differentiates itself by focusing on high-complexity systems that standard electricians avoid.

*   **Accredited Programming:** Professional commissioning of Clipsal C-Bus, Philips Dynalite, and DALI systems.
*   **System Rescue & Audits:** Taking over "broken" or half-finished projects from other contractors and restoring full functionality.
*   **Modernization (The "Bridge" Service):** Integrating legacy wired systems with Apple HomeKit, Google Home, and Alexa for modern UX.
*   **Commercial Compliance:** DALI-2 compliance, AFSS emergency lighting testing, and energy-saving audits for commercial buildings.

## 4. Strategic Growth Plan (2026)
### Phase 1: Strategic Partnerships (Q1-Q2)
Establish "Technical Partner" status with 5-10 key luxury builders and electrical contractors who outsource their automation programming.
### Phase 2: Targeted Lead Generation (Q2-Q3)
Deploy automated lead scraping and outreach targeting architects and developers in high-growth Sydney corridors (CBD, Macquarie Park, Parramatta).
### Phase 3: Service Contract Model (Q3-Q4)
Transition commercial clients to recurring maintenance contracts for lighting control and emergency lighting compliance to stabilize cash flow.

## 5. Marketing & Positioning
*   **Authority Positioning:** Leverage the "Dual Accreditation" (C-Bus + Dynalite) as a unique selling proposition.
*   **Local SEO:** Maintain and expand the existing hyper-local landing page strategy (e.g., "C-Bus Repair Mosman", "Dynalite Specialist CBD").
*   **Case Studies:** Document "Rescue Projects" where Sydney Automation Co. solved complex issues that other contractors couldn't.

## 6. Financial Goals
*   **Primary Revenue:** High-margin programming and fault-finding call-outs.
*   **Secondary Revenue:** Hardware sales (SpaceLogic, Dynalite controllers) and recurring maintenance contracts.
*   **Target:** 25% year-on-year growth through expanded commercial maintenance and high-end residential project partnerships.
