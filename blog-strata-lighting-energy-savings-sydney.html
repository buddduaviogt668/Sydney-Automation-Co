Lighting accounts for 20–40% of commercial and strata building energy consumption in Australia. For strata managers and building owners in Sydney, this represents one of the most accessible areas for significant cost reduction — without compromising the comfort or safety of residents and tenants.

At Sydney Automation Co., we've delivered lighting automation upgrades across hundreds of strata buildings, commercial towers, and mixed-use properties across Greater Sydney. Here's what actually works.

How Occupancy-Based Control Delivers the Biggest Savings

The single most impactful change you can make in a strata building is occupancy-based zone control. C-Bus PIR sensors automatically detect whether a space is occupied and dim or switch off lighting in empty zones.

  • Common corridors, lobbies, and car parks see 40–60% energy reduction
  • C-Bus PIR sensors are adjustable from 5–30 minute timeouts — balancing savings and resident comfort
  • Compatible with existing C-Bus and Dynalite infrastructure — no rewiring required in most cases
  • Fault-tolerant: if a sensor fails, lights default to ON for safety

Daylight Harvesting with C-Bus Light Sensors

C-Bus 5750L photocell sensors continuously measure ambient natural light levels and automatically reduce artificial lighting output in response. In perimeter zones with significant window exposure, this typically delivers:

  • 30–50% reduction in perimeter zone lighting during daylight hours
  • Seamless dimming — residents and tenants rarely notice the adjustment
  • Fully programmable setpoints via C-Bus Toolkit — no guesswork

Scheduled Dimming with Astronomical Time Clocks

Dynalite and C-Bus both support astronomical time clock scheduling, which adjusts lighting scenes based on the actual time of sunrise and sunset for your location. Practical applications include:

  • Corridors dim to 30% after 7PM and on weekends — saving 15–25% with zero comfort impact
  • Car park lighting reduces to minimum safe levels during low-traffic overnight periods
  • External facade and feature lighting operates only during relevant hours

NABERS Rating and NSW ESS Compliance Benefits

The NABERS Energy rating scheme directly rewards buildings with automated lighting control. Improved NABERS scores increase property valuations and attract higher-quality commercial tenants.

Additionally, the NSW Government Energy Savings Scheme (ESS) provides financial incentives for qualifying commercial lighting upgrades. We assist strata managers and building owners in identifying applicable rebates and preparing required documentation. With available incentives, typical payback periods reduce from 2.3 years to under 18 months.

C-Bus DALI-2 compliant systems also satisfy NCC 2025 energy efficiency requirements, future-proofing your building against upcoming regulatory changes.

Real Energy Monitoring — Know Your Savings

C-Bus energy monitoring modules measure real-time power consumption per circuit, providing building managers with actual consumption data rather than estimates. Benefits include:

  • Identify circuits with unusual consumption indicating faults or inefficiencies
  • Generate reports for body corporate meetings demonstrating actual cost savings
  • Detect lighting degradation early — before bulbs fail completely
  • Typical payback period for monitoring-only upgrades: 12–18 months

Frequently Asked Questions

How much can we realistically save on strata lighting?
Based on our Sydney projects, strata buildings typically achieve 30–55% reduction in lighting energy costs. The biggest gains come from car parks and corridors where lights were previously running 24/7. A typical 40-unit building saves $8,000–$18,000 per year depending on existing infrastructure.
Do we need to replace our existing C-Bus or Dynalite system?
Usually not. C-Bus and Dynalite systems are highly modular — we can add occupancy sensors, daylight harvesting, and scheduling to most existing installations without replacing the core infrastructure. This dramatically reduces upgrade costs compared to a full system replacement.
How long does an energy upgrade take to install?
For most strata buildings, the installation and programming is completed in 1–2 days with minimal disruption to residents. We schedule work to avoid common-area downtime during peak morning and evening periods.
Do you provide reports for body corporate meetings?
Yes. We provide a full written report covering the scope of work, expected energy savings, ESS rebate eligibility, and a clear payback calculation. This is designed to be presented directly at body corporate and strata management meetings.
Are you accredited for C-Bus and Dynalite work?
Yes. Sydney Automation Co. holds current accreditations from Clipsal (Schneider Electric) for C-Bus and Signify (Philips) for Dynalite. We use manufacturer-level diagnostic software and are fully licensed for electrical work in NSW.
