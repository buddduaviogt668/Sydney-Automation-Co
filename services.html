Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about C-Bus, Dynalite and lighting control

What's the difference between C-Bus and Dynalite? ▼
C-Bus and Dynalite are both Clipsal systems but target different market segments. C-Bus is modular and scalable for residential to large commercial applications. Dynalite is more specialized for legacy commercial and retrofit applications needing emergency lighting integration. Both offer excellent control and programmability.
How long does a typical system installation take? ▼
Installation time varies based on system complexity and building size. Typical residential systems take 2-5 days. Larger commercial installations can take 1-4 weeks. We always provide a detailed timeline during the design consultation phase.
Can I connect my C-Bus system to smart home platforms? ▼
Yes! Modern C-Bus systems integrate with Apple HomeKit, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, and other platforms. We design systems with future connectivity in mind and handle all the integration setup for you.
What if something breaks after installation? ▼
All our installations include a 12-month warranty on workmanship. We also offer same-day or next-day fault-finding and repair service across all of Sydney. Most issues are resolved within hours.
Do you service systems installed by other companies? ▼
Absolutely. We service, maintain and repair C-Bus and Dynalite systems regardless of who installed them. We also troubleshoot and fix issues that other electricians couldn't solve.

Not sure what you need?

Call us and describe the system. We'll tell you exactly what's involved and give you a fixed price.
