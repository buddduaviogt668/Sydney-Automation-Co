FAQs

Questions Answered by
Our Specialists

What systems does your lighting automation company service in Sydney? +
We focus exclusively on lighting control and building automation protocols. This includes Clipsal C-Bus, Philips Dynalite, DALI-2, and RAPIX systems. We do not do industrial factory automation or IT systems; our expertise is 100% dedicated to commercial and high-end residential lighting automation.
Are your technicians accredited? +
Yes, we hold manufacturer-level accreditations from Clipsal (Schneider Electric) for C-Bus, Signify (Philips) for Dynalite, and are certified for DALI-2 systems. George Skarmoutsos has over 15 years of industry experience, including time working in Clipsal National Technical Support.
Do you offer emergency on-site service? +
Yes. We understand that a lighting control crash in a commercial tower or strata block is a critical issue. We offer priority same-day response for urgent faults across Greater Sydney, and our vans are stocked with common C-Bus and Dynalite replacement parts.
