OC Not Signed Off?
AFSS Due?
Don't Risk It.

In NSW, failing to maintain compliant emergency lighting can result in your Annual Fire Safety Statement being rejected, your Occupation Certificate being withheld, or your building being issued a fire safety order by the council. The consequences are serious — fines, delays, and potential liability.

We work to your deadline. Whether you have a week or a day, we mobilise fast, identify every fault, and provide the documentation your certifier, council, or developer needs.

Common Questions

Emergency Lighting &
Compliance FAQ

Do you work on RAPIX Emergency lighting systems? +
Yes. Sydney Automation Co. are RAPIX specialists and work on RAPIX Emergency systems across hotels, train stations, commercial buildings, strata complexes, clubs, pubs, RSLs and shopping centres throughout Greater Sydney.
Can you help us pass our AFSS? +
Yes. We identify and repair faults in emergency lighting systems — including failed spitfires, dead exit signs and faulty emergency battens — to ensure your building passes the Annual Fire Safety Statement (AFSS) requirements under NSW legislation.
What is the difference between OC, PC and AFSS? +
OC (Occupation Certificate) and PC (Practical Completion) are required at the end of a construction project, confirming all essential services including emergency lighting are compliant. AFSS (Annual Fire Safety Statement) is an ongoing annual certification required by NSW councils for all buildings with essential fire safety measures. All three require your emergency lighting to be tested, documented and operational.
Do you work with our existing electricians? +
Absolutely. We regularly work alongside building electricians, providing specialist RAPIX and emergency lighting expertise while their team handles general electrical work. We can also bring our own licensed electricians if required.
What emergency lighting faults do you fix? +
We fix failed spitfire emergency lights, dead or dim exit signs, faulty emergency battens, RAPIX Emergency system faults, Schneider emergency system issues, and any emergency lighting that is preventing your building from passing compliance testing.
Do you service all of Sydney? +
Yes. We service all of Greater Sydney including the CBD, Eastern Suburbs, North Shore, Northern Beaches, Western Sydney, Sutherland Shire, Parramatta, and surrounding regions. We also service the Illawarra, Southern Highlands and Central Coast for larger projects.
