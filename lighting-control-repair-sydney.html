Why Repair, Not Replace

Repair Is Almost Always
the Right Answer

Lighting control systems are designed to last 20-30 years with proper maintenance. When a fault occurs, it's almost always a single failed module, not the whole system.

  • Module replacement costs a fraction of full system replacement
  • Existing cabling and infrastructure remains intact
  • Programming and scenes are preserved or restored
  • No need to repaint, patch walls or re-run cabling
  • Repairs are completed in hours, not days
  • Older systems are still fully supported and serviceable

FAQs

Lighting Control Repair
Questions Answered

What lighting control systems can you repair in Sydney? +
We repair all major lighting control systems including Clipsal C-Bus, Philips Dynalite, DALI-2, RAPIX and most other commercial and residential automation platforms. We are C-Bus accredited, Dynalite accredited and DALI-2 certified.
How do I know if my lighting system needs repair or replacement? +
In most cases, repair is the right answer. Lighting control systems are modular and individual failed components can be replaced without touching the rest of the system. Full replacement is expensive and rarely necessary. A diagnostic visit will give you a clear answer within the first hour.
Can you repair lighting control systems you didn't install? +
Yes — the majority of our repair work is on systems we did not originally install. We do not require you to have been our client previously. Bring us any C-Bus, Dynalite, DALI or RAPIX system and we will diagnose and repair it.
How do I know what lighting control system my building has? +
Call us and describe what you see — wall panel appearance, enclosure location, any visible branding on keypads or the switchboard. We can usually identify the system from a description or a phone photo before we arrive, so we can bring the right tools and parts.
