What We Fix

C-Bus Faults We
Repair Every Day

Most C-Bus faults are caused by a small set of recurring failures. As accredited specialists, we diagnose and repair all of them — usually on the first visit.

  • Dimmer module failures — lights not responding or flickering
  • C-Bus network communication errors (no response on network)
  • Keypad and input unit failures — buttons not working
  • Relay and output unit faults — circuits stuck on or off
  • Programming corruption — scenes and schedules not triggering
  • Power supply unit failures
  • C-Bus PC Interface (PCIA) and CNI faults
  • Logic gate and trigger programming errors
  • C-Bus touchscreen (C-Touch) faults
  • Post-renovation system damage and rewiring faults

C-Bus Repair Questions Answered

How much does C-Bus repair cost in Sydney? +
C-Bus repair costs vary by fault type and time on-site. Most diagnostic visits are 1-2 hours. Simple faults like keypad replacements or power supply issues are typically resolved on the first visit. Network-wide and programming faults may require additional time. We provide a clear scope and quote before any work proceeds — no surprises.
Can you repair C-Bus systems installed by someone else? +
Yes — the majority of our C-Bus repair work is on systems installed by other Automation Specialists. We are Clipsal C-Bus accredited and can access, diagnose and repair any C-Bus installation regardless of who originally installed or programmed it.
How quickly can you respond to a C-Bus fault in Sydney? +
We offer same-day response for urgent C-Bus faults across Greater Sydney. Call 0422 469 739 to check current availability. Priority bookings are available for commercial buildings and strata complexes.
What are the most common C-Bus faults you repair? +
The most common C-Bus faults we see in Sydney: dimmer module failures, C-Bus network communication errors, keypad and input unit failures, relay and output unit faults, programming corruption, and power supply failures. Most are diagnosed and repaired on the first visit.
Do you carry C-Bus replacement parts? +
We carry the most common C-Bus replacement modules and components. For less common or discontinued units, we source parts quickly through our Clipsal supply chain. We'll advise on part availability before you commit to a repair.
Can you upgrade my C-Bus system while repairing it? +
Yes. Many clients take the opportunity to upgrade scenes, schedules, dimmer curves or add automation features while we're on-site for a repair. This is cost-effective since the system is already open and diagnosed. Ask us what's possible for your install.

"My C-Bus lights aren't responding at all in Paddington — is it the programming or a hardware fault?"

This is the most common call we get from Paddington. Nine times out of ten, it's one of three things: a corrupted group address in the C-Bus network, a failed relay module, or a communication dropout on the bus cable. When we arrive at a terrace house near Oxford Street, the first thing we do is plug in the C-Bus toolkit and read the live network — within 10–15 minutes we can usually tell you exactly which unit is at fault and whether it can be fixed on the spot or needs a part ordered. We carry the most common C-Bus modules in the van, so most Paddington jobs are resolved same-day.

"How long does a C-Bus repair or reprogramming visit in Paddington actually take?"

For a straightforward reprogramming job — say, scenes not working properly or a new switch that needs to be commissioned — plan for 2–3 hours at your terrace house. Fault-finding jobs, especially in older Paddington buildings where the c-bus cabling hasn't been touched in 10+ years, can run 3–5 hours if there's physical bus damage involved. We'll always give you a clear estimate before we start and call you if we find something unexpected mid-job. No surprises.

"Can you reprogram my C-Bus system without replacing any hardware in Paddington?"

Absolutely — and this is actually the most satisfying part of the job. We've had Paddington clients who were quoted full system replacements by other tradespeople, only to find out the entire issue was a scene that had been accidentally overwritten or a unit ID conflict that takes 20 minutes to fix in software. George has been programming C-Bus systems since the Clipsal national support days, so he knows every quirk of the platform. If it can be fixed in software, we'll fix it in software — no unnecessary hardware costs.

"Who do I call in Paddington when my C-Bus system stops working after hours or on a weekend?"

Call us directly on 0422 469 739. We cover Paddington and surrounding areas including Oxford Street for emergency callouts. We prioritise situations where you've lost control of essential lighting — security lights, common area lights in strata buildings, or systems that have simply gone dark. We don't outsource to a call centre; you'll speak directly to George, who makes the call on whether we can diagnose remotely via the C-Bus toolkit or need to be on-site same day.
